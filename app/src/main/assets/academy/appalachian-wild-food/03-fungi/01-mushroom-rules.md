Most wild mushrooms are harmless or merely bad. A few are deadly, and some deadly ones look just like good ones. Mushrooms are not worth the risk until you have been taught by an expert in person.

## Rules
1. **Never eat a mushroom you cannot name with certainty.**
2. **Take a spore print and learn the deadly groups first** (Amanita, Galerina, Lepiota, Cortinarius, Gyromitra).
3. **Cook every wild mushroom.** Many cause illness when raw.
4. **Eat one new species at a time and keep a sample.**
5. **Never eat any mushroom with white gills, a ring on the stem and a bulb or cup at the base.** That is the pattern of the deadly Amanitas.

## A short list of beginner-safe mushrooms in the Appalachians
- **Morel (true morel):** a honeycomb cap that is **hollow from tip to base** when sliced. Spring, around dying elms, tulip poplar and old apple trees. **False morels** (Gyromitra) are brainlike, wrinkled and cottony inside; they are poisonous.
- **Chicken of the woods (sulphur shelf):** bright orange and yellow shelves on oak. Eat only young, tender edges. Avoid specimens on conifers, eucalyptus or other trees; a few people react.
- **Hen of the woods (maitake):** grey-brown frilly clusters at the base of oaks in fall.
- **Giant puffball:** white inside all through, like marshmallow. Cut it open: any shape inside means it is not a puffball.
- **Chanterelle:** orange-gold, with blunt, forking ridges instead of true gills. **Look-alike: jack-o'-lantern**, which has true, thin gills, grows in clusters on wood and is poisonous.

## If someone is sick
Call poison control at **1-800-222-1222** and go to the hospital. Take the mushroom with you.

## Check yourself

? What pattern marks the deadly Amanita mushrooms?
- A honeycomb cap
- Orange shelves on wood
- Being white
+ White gills, a ring on the stem, and a bulb or cup at the base
> This pattern has killed many foragers.

? How do you tell a true morel from a false morel?
- It is brown
- It grows in spring
- It smells sweet
+ A true morel is hollow from tip to base
> False morels are cottony or chambered inside.

? What should you do with every wild mushroom?
- Eat it raw
+ Cook it
- Dry it only
- Boil it for 30 seconds
> Raw wild mushrooms often cause illness.
