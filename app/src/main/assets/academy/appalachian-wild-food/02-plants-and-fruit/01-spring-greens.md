Early spring is the hungry gap: stored food is low and the garden has not started. Wild greens fill it.

## Safe starters
- **Dandelion:** the whole plant is edible. Young leaves are less bitter; boil older ones in a change of water. **Look-alike:** hawkweed and cat's ear are also edible. The true dandelion has a single hollow flower stalk with no leaves.
- **Chickweed:** a low, sprawling plant with tiny star flowers and a single line of hairs on the stem. Eaten raw or cooked.
- **Wild garlic and ramps (leeks):** found in rich, damp woods. The leaves smell strongly of onion or garlic. **Never eat a plant that does not smell of onion.** Deadly **lily of the valley** and **false hellebore** (corn lily) have similar leaves in spring but no onion smell. Take only one leaf from each plant, and check protected-plant rules, because ramps are over-harvested.
- **Violet, plantain and lamb's quarters** are common and mild.
- **Cattail shoots** (white cores of young stalks) taste like cucumber. Only gather from clean water.

## Plants to treat as dangerous
- **Pokeweed** is a poisonous plant. Experienced people boil young shoots in several waters, but mistakes and mature stems kill. **Skip it unless taught in person.**
- **Water hemlock** (spotted stem, umbrella-shaped flowers) is one of the deadliest plants in North America. Never eat any wild plant with umbrella-shaped flower clusters unless you know it with certainty.

## Cooking
Boil greens for 5 to 10 minutes and drain. Add salt and any fat you have.

## Check yourself

? What proves a plant is a ramp or wild garlic rather than a deadly look-alike?
- It is green
- It is in the woods
- It grows in spring
+ It smells like onion or garlic
> Smell is the key check; the poisonous look-alikes do not smell of onion.

? Which plant should you skip unless taught in person?
- Dandelion
- Chickweed
- Plantain
+ Pokeweed
> Poke is toxic when mistakes are made.

? Where should you NOT collect cattail shoots?
+ From polluted or roadside water
- From clean ponds
- From streams in the woods
- Anywhere is fine
> Cattails absorb what is in the water.
