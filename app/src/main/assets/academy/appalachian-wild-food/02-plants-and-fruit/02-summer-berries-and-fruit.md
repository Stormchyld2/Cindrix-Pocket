Berries give sugar and vitamins and are easy to learn. Some wild berries are poisonous, so rules still apply.

## Safe, common berries
- **Blackberry and raspberry:** thorny canes, compound leaves, clusters of dark or red berries that pull off leaving a hollow core.
- **Blueberry and huckleberry:** low shrubs in the mountains, with round blue berries that have a five-pointed crown.
- **Wild strawberry:** a small strawberry, trailing runners, three-toothed leaves.
- **Serviceberry (Juneberry, sarvis):** a small tree with blue-purple berries in June.
- **Mulberry:** a tree with dark berries, sweet. Eat ripe ones.
- **Elderberry:** ripe dark purple berries from clusters. **Cook them. Raw berries, leaves, bark and stems cause sickness.**
- **Pawpaw** (late summer into fall): a large green or yellowing fruit that tastes like custard. Skins and seeds are not eaten. A few people react; start with a small amount.

## Poisonous look-alikes and berries to avoid
- **Pokeberries:** dark purple berries on red stems. Poisonous.
- **Virginia creeper:** five-leaflet vine with blue berries. Poisonous.
- **Nightshades, baneberry (white or red berries), moonseed (looks like a wild grape, with a single crescent seed) and snowberry.** Skip any berry you cannot name.
- **Rule:** wild grapes have forked tendrils and several seeds; moonseed has a smooth vine, no tendrils and one seed.

## Keeping them
Dry thin slices of berries on screens or crush and cook into jam if you have sugar or honey.

## Check yourself

? How can you tell a wild grape from moonseed?
- Moonseed has tendrils
- Both are fine to eat
+ Grapes have forked tendrils and several seeds
- Moonseed is red
> Moonseed has no tendrils and one crescent seed.

? What should you do with elderberries?
- Eat the leaves too
- Eat them raw by the handful
+ Cook them before eating
- Drink the bark tea
> Raw berries and other parts cause sickness.

? Which berry is poisonous?
- Blackberry
- Blueberry
- Serviceberry
+ Pokeberry
> Poke berries on red stems are toxic.
