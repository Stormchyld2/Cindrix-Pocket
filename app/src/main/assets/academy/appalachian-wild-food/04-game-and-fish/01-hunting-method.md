Hunting is mostly patience and knowing the animal. The skill is in being where the animal will be, unseen and unsmelled.

## Before you go
- Pass **hunter education** and buy your licence and tags.
- Tell someone where you will be and when you will be back.
- **Wear blaze orange** during gun seasons. It is the law in many seasons, and it saves lives.
- Scout in the off season: look for tracks, droppings, beds, rubs, scrapes, trails, and feeding areas.

## Method
- **Wind:** keep it in your face or crosswise. Animals smell you long before they see you.
- **Sit (stand hunting):** the most reliable way. Use a tree stand safely with a harness or a ground blind, near a trail, water or food, and sit still. Get in before first light.
- **Still-hunting:** move slowly, a few steps at a time, then stop and watch for a long time. Move into the wind.
- **Stalk:** move only when the animal is feeding or looking away. Take shorter steps.
- **Calling:** turkey and predator calls work in season and with practice.

## Know your target and what is beyond it
Never shoot at a sound or a movement. Identify the animal and be sure of a safe backstop. A bullet can travel over a mile.

## Safe in a group
Never swing a firearm toward another person. Agree on shooting zones. If you are using a stand, tell others where it is.
