Use this calendar as a guide. Seasons start earlier in the valleys and later on the ridges. The Calendar screen shows "In season now" from the same list.

- **March to April:** dandelion, chickweed, violets, early greens, ramps (check rules), cattail shoots, trout fishing opens. Wild turkey in spring.
- **May to June:** morels (spring), serviceberry, strawberries, mulberries, young greens, bluegill spawning.
- **July to August:** blackberries, raspberries, blueberries, huckleberries, elderberries (cook them), chicken of the woods, summer vegetables in the garden.
- **September to October:** black walnuts, hickory nuts, acorns, pawpaws, wild grapes, apples, persimmons (after frost), hen of the woods, deer and squirrel seasons.
- **November to December:** nuts finishing, persimmons, deer season, rabbits and squirrels, dig Jerusalem artichokes and cattail roots after frost.
- **January to February:** stored food, small game and fish, tracking, planning the garden. Little to forage.

## Keep a book
Each year, write down when you found each food, where, and how much. In three years you have a map that no guide can match.

## Plan around the gap
The poorest months are late winter and early spring. Store food for them, and plant early greens in the garden.
