Boots decide how far you can walk. A cobbler's skills extend their life by years.

## Tools
Awl, heavy waxed thread (or artificial sinew), two needles, scissors or a sharp knife, contact cement, a hammer and a flat stone.

## Common repairs
- **Torn stitching:** clean out the old thread, use an awl to re-pierce the holes, and sew with a **saddle stitch**: two needles, one on each end of a single thread, passing through the same holes in opposite directions.
- **Worn sole:** clean both surfaces, rough them, apply contact cement to both, let it get tacky, press hard and clamp for a day. Stitch around the edge for a lasting repair.
- **Cracked leather:** clean it, rub in a leather dressing or beeswax.
- **Broken laces:** replace with cord or leather.

## Care
Clean boots after use, dry them slowly away from direct heat, and treat leather with wax or oil. Never dry boots by a fire. Heat shrinks and cracks the leather.

## Scraps
Keep old belts, boots and straps. Leather is useful for patches, hinges, straps, and soles.
