A needle, thread and thimble can keep a family clothed for years.

## Basic stitches
- **Running stitch:** in and out in a line. For seams and quick joins.
- **Backstitch:** strongest. Each stitch goes back to the end of the last one.
- **Whip stitch:** over the edge, for joining or patching.
- **Blanket stitch:** edges and button holes.

## A patch
1. Cut a patch larger than the hole, with the edges folded under.
2. Pin it over the hole.
3. Stitch around the edge with small, even stitches.
4. Stitch the inside edge of the hole to the patch.

## Buttons and seams
Sew buttons with a shank of thread so the button sits above the cloth. Re-sew a seam by following the old stitch holes.

## Thread and cloth
Waxed or heavy thread suits canvas, leather and tents. Save good cloth from worn-out clothes. Keep a repair kit with needles of different sizes, thread, patches and safety pins.

## Safety
Never leave needles loose where children or pets can reach them.
