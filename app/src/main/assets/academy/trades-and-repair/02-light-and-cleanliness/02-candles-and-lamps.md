Light extends the day, but fire from candles and lamps burns houses down.

## Candles
- **Materials:** beeswax or tallow (rendered fat), a wick of twisted cotton or string, and a mould or dipping can.
- **Dipping:** dip the wick into melted wax, let it cool, and repeat to build layers.
- **Rushlights:** dip dry rushes in fat and clip them in a holder.

## Oil lamps
- **Fuel:** vegetable oil, lamp oil or lard, with a cotton wick through a floating cork or holder.
- Never use gasoline or other volatile fuels.
- Use a glass chimney, because it makes a steadier, cleaner flame.

## Safety
- Never leave a flame unattended.
- Keep it on a stable, non-flammable surface away from curtains, bedding and paper.
- Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby.
- Do not use candles or lamps in a tent or a closed space overnight.
- Keep a **carbon monoxide** alarm if you burn anything indoors for long.

## Better options
Use battery or crank lamps for sleeping areas and flame only when necessary.
