Four knots do almost everything. Learn them until your hands know them in the dark.

## Four knots
- **Square knot:** joins two ends of equal rope (bandages, bundles).
- **Bowline:** a loop that will not slip and can be untied after a load.
- **Clove hitch:** fastens a rope to a pole quickly.
- **Taut-line hitch:** an adjustable loop for tent lines.

## Making cord
Strong, flexible stems and inner barks make cordage. Nettle, dogbane, milkweed, basswood and cattail leaf all work. Peel, dry, then **reverse-wrap**: twist two strands in the same direction, then wrap them together in the opposite direction so the cord holds. Test it with your full weight before relying on it.

## Taking care of rope
Keep it dry, out of the sun, and clean. Never use a frayed rope for a load that matters. Tie a stopper knot in the end so it does not run.

## Do it
- [ ] Tie all four knots without looking, three times each.
- [ ] Make one foot of plant cord and test it by hanging from it with a pack on.
