Rescuers cannot save someone they cannot see. Signal early, then decide when to move.

## Signals that work
- **Three of anything:** three whistle blasts, three shouts, three fires, three flashes of a mirror. Three is the world distress signal.
- **Whistle:** carries farther than a voice and takes no energy.
- **Mirror:** reflects the sun for miles. Sweep it across the horizon.
- **Fire:** use smoke by day (add green leaves) and flame by night.
- **Ground signal:** a big X or SOS in contrasting colour on open ground, at least ten feet long.
- **Bright cloth** hung high.

## If a plane or helicopter is near
Make yourself big and bright, stand in the open, and wave both arms. Do not point a mirror at a pilot's eyes.

## Moving
Move only for a good reason. If you go, mark the trail, leave a note at the old camp, follow a stream downhill toward people, travel in daylight and rest often.

## Do it
- [ ] Make a ground signal at least ten feet long using what is around you.
- [ ] Practise three whistle blasts, a pause, and three more.

## Check yourself

? What is the world distress signal?
- One long blast
- Two flashes
- Silence
+ Three of anything
> Three whistles, fires or shouts mean help.

? How should you signal with smoke by day?
- Burn dry paper
- Use no fire
+ Add green leaves or damp material for white smoke
- Put the fire out
> Thick white smoke shows against a green forest.

? When you move on, you should
- Burn the camp
+ Leave a note and mark your trail
- Say nothing
- Move at night only
> A note tells searchers where you went.
