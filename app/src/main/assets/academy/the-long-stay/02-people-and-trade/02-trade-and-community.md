No household can make everything. The group that trades well lives better.

## What to trade
Skills first (repairs, medical help, teaching), then goods that keep: salt, soap, candles, seeds, tools, preserved food, firewood, hand-made goods.

## How
- Meet in the open, in daylight, at a regular place.
- Agree a fair rate in goods you both value.
- Do not trade away your last critical supply.
- Be honest. A good name is worth more than one deal.

## Neighbours
Know them now. Share information, watch together and help the weak. A cooperating street is safer and healthier than a locked house.

## Risks
Trade can bring thieves and disease. Keep a safe distance, wash after meeting, and never go alone.

## Check yourself

? What is worth most to trade?
+ Skills and goods that keep
- Fresh meat in summer
- Paper money only
- Nothing
> Skills and durable goods hold value.

? Where should you trade?
- In a dark alley
- Alone at night
+ In the open, in daylight
- Inside a stranger's home
> Safety first.

? What should you not trade away?
- Extra seed
+ Your last critical supply
- Spare tools
- Surplus honey
> Never trade away what you cannot replace.
