Security is not a one-night job. It is a routine that lasts.

## Layers
1. **Be unremarkable:** do not advertise what you have.
2. **See early:** a watch, lights, noise makers, and a dog.
3. **Slow people down:** locks, fences, hedges and gates.
4. **Be ready to respond** with a plan, a rally point, and a safe room.
5. **Know the law** and the limits on force.

## Watch
Use the Security Operations screens to plan shifts so no one is exhausted, with a log and handovers. Never leave a post unmanned.

## Communication
Agree signals for danger and all-clear, with radios, whistles or lights. Practise.

## People
Know your neighbours, and who is welcome. Plan for strangers asking for help: how you will talk, what you will give, and from where.

## The goal
Go home safe. Avoid conflict whenever you can. The best defence is not being the easy target.
