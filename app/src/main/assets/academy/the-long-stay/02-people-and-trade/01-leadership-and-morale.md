Most groups in a long emergency fail from people problems, not supply problems.

## Roles
Decide who leads, who does what, and how decisions are made. Write it down. Rotate hard jobs. Use skills, not rank.

## Work and rest
Set a daily schedule with fair shares of the hard jobs. Make sure everyone has time to rest. Watch for burnout and grief.

## Disputes
Settle small quarrels early and in the open. Agree on rules and consequences before trouble starts. A fair process matters more than who wins.

## Children, the old and the sick
Give them work they can do, protect them and include them. They need routine and safety.

## Morale
Keep traditions: shared meals, music, stories, faith, games. Mark the end of the week and the seasons.

## Warning signs
Hoarding, secrecy, blame, and a person who stops eating or speaking. Talk to them early and kindly. If someone talks of ending their life, stay with them and get help: in the US call or text **988**.
