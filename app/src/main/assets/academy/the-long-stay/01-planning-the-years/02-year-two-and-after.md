If the emergency lasts, the second year is a test of whether you built something that sustains itself.

## Close the loops
- **Seeds:** save seed from your best plants.
- **Livestock:** a small flock of chickens or goats gives eggs, milk, meat and manure, and needs feed.
- **Fuel:** a woodlot you cut sustainably.
- **Tools:** repair, sharpen and replace handles. Learn blacksmithing, woodwork and leather.
- **Medicine:** herbs you know and a way to treat what you can treat.

## Skills pass on
Teach children and neighbours. A skill known by one person is a single point of failure.

## Trade
Specialise a little. A neighbour who mends shoes, makes soap or fixes engines is more valuable than a pile of cans.

## Health and morale
Keep rest days, shared meals, and time for play, music and prayer.

## Review
Every season, review what you have, what you used, what failed, and what to change.
