A long emergency is won in the first year by doing a small number of things in the right order.

## The principles that shape it
These come from emergency management: **prevention**, **preparedness**, **response**, **recovery** and **mitigation**. In a long stay they become: protect what you have, build what you will need, respond to each day's problems, recover from losses and reduce the next ones.

## First days
Make everyone safe, count people, water, food, fuel and medicine, and agree who decides.

## First month
Set a daily routine, ration food, set a watch, make safe water and sanitation, and talk to neighbours.

## Months 2 to 6
Plant a garden as soon as the season allows, preserve every surplus, get the wood in, mend what is broken, and work with neighbours.

## Months 6 to 12
Rebuild stocks, run a full growing and harvest season, and train others. Keep records. Write down what worked.

## What to avoid
Running out of one critical thing, such as water or fuel, because nobody watched the count. Burnout. Splitting the group. Taking big risks for small gains.
