Sickness spreads fast when water and waste are not managed. Sanitation prevents more disease than medicine in a long stay.

## Water and waste
- Treat all drinking water. Keep it covered and clean.
- Dig latrines at least 200 feet from water, downhill of wells, and cover waste daily with soil or ash.
- Wash hands with soap and clean water after the toilet and before eating or preparing food.
- Burn or bury trash. Control flies and rodents.

## Food safety
Keep food covered and cold when you can. Cook meat well. Do not eat spoiled food.

## Teeth and mouth
Brush with a soft brush and baking soda or salt. Chew hard foods and avoid sugar. Tooth infections can kill. Keep a dental kit and learn to handle common problems.

## Sleep and fitness
Rest, regular meals and daily work keep people well. Do not skip sleep to do more.

## Medical supplies
Stock what you can use, and rely on the Academy's medical courses. Never guess at doses: follow labels, or ask a doctor or pharmacist.

## Keep records
A health log: illnesses, injuries, and who has what condition.
