## Keep records
Write down what you have, what you use and what you plant. Keep a log of events, weather and visitors. Records let you plan and settle arguments.

## Teach
Pair each skill holder with a learner. Run drills, record lessons, and sign off skills in the Academy. Aim for **two people who can do every must-have skill.**

## Review and adapt
Meet at the end of each season. List three things that worked, three that did not, and three changes.

## Knowing when to move
Move only if staying is more dangerous than going: fire, flood, contamination, violence, or a lack of water you cannot fix. Plan your route, your destination, the supplies you carry and your rally points before you need them. If you must move, go early, in daylight, with a plan.

## The long view
A group that keeps good records, trains its people and reviews its work gets better every year.
