Fish are the easiest wild protein to get repeatedly.

## Hooks and lines
- Keep a spare line and some hooks in every kit.
- **Improvised hooks:** a safety pin, a bent pin or a thorn. A **gorge** (a pointed stick set in bait) works for swallowing fish.
- **Bait:** worms, grubs, grasshoppers, bits of meat.

## Other ways (check the law)
- **Hand lines and set lines** hung from branches over deep water.
- **Stone fish weirs** in a stream.
- **Spear** with a sharpened stick in shallow clear water: aim below the fish, because light bends.
- **Crayfish, mussels and frogs** where legal; cook well.

## Where
Deep, shaded pools, behind rocks, under overhanging banks, and at the mouth of feeder streams. Fish early and late.

## Care
Clean fish at once, cook to 145°F, and eat the same day or dry or smoke it. Do not eat fish with open sores or from water with chemical smell.

## Check yourself

? Where do fish like to hide?
+ Deep shaded pools and under banks
- In fast open water
- In dry creek beds
- On the surface only
> They want cover and slow water.

? How do you aim a spear at a fish in the water?
- Above it
- At it directly
+ Below where it appears
- Behind it
> Light bends at the surface.

? When should you clean fish?
+ At once
- The next day
- After a week
- Never
> Fish spoil quickly.
