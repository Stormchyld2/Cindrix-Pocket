A grown adult needs roughly 2,000 to 3,000 calories a day, more with hard work in the cold. Wild food rarely gives that unless you know it well.

## What gives the most food for the effort
1. **Nuts and acorns:** high in fat and calories, easy to store.
2. **Fish in a pond or stream:** a steady, repeatable source.
3. **Small game (squirrel, rabbit):** fast to get, small each, plentiful.
4. **Deer and larger game:** one animal feeds a family for months.
5. **Greens and berries:** vitamins, not calories.
6. **Bugs:** safe, calorie-dense and easy if cooked, but not a main food.

## Fat is the problem
Lean wild meat without fat can cause "rabbit starvation" (sickness from too much lean protein). Pair meat with fat, nuts, grain or starches.

## Plan
Do not rely on foraging for a household. Treat it as a supplement to a garden, stored food, small livestock and traded goods. Learn it now when it is easy.
