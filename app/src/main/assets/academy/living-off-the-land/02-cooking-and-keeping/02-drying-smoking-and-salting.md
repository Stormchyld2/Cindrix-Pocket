Without a freezer, meat and fish keep by removing water or adding salt and smoke.

## Drying
- Cut **lean** meat in strips about a quarter inch thick, with the grain for chewy jerky.
- Dry at about 140–160°F, in a low oven, over a smoky fire or on racks in the sun and wind, until it bends and cracks but does not snap.
- Store it dry in airtight containers.
- Fat spoils, so trim it.

## Salting
- Bury meat in coarse salt or soak in a strong brine (salt strong enough to float an egg).
- Salt for several days, then dry. Heavily salted meat must be soaked before cooking.
- Never use too little salt.

## Smoking
Smoke adds flavour and helps protect meat. Hang strips or small pieces in a cool smoke (not hot) for a day or more after salting. Use hardwood, not pine or other resinous woods.

## Fish
Split and salt, then smoke or dry. Keep it free from flies.

## Check and store
Look weekly for mould, odd smells, or sliminess. Throw out anything that smells off. When in doubt, throw it out.

## Check yourself

? Which meat dries best?
- Fatty meat
- Fresh liver
+ Lean meat
- Wet meat
> Fat turns rancid.

? What wood for smoking?
- Pine
+ Hardwood such as hickory or apple
- Painted scrap wood
- Any wood
> Resinous or treated woods spoil flavour and may be harmful.

? How do you test brine?
+ It should float an egg
- It tastes sweet
- It turns clear
- It is cold
> A strong brine floats an egg.
