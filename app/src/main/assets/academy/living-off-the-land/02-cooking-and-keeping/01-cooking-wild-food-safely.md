Wild meat and fish carry germs and parasites that cooking kills.

## Temperatures (use a thermometer)
- Ground meat and sausage: **160°F**.
- Poultry, turkey and small game: **165°F**.
- Whole cuts of venison, beef-like game and fish: **145°F**, then a 3 minute rest.
- Bear, wild pig and raccoon: cook well done, because of the risk of trichinosis (**160°F or more, no pink**).

## Methods without a stove
- **Boiling and stewing:** good for tough meat and for saving fat and nutrients.
- **Roasting on a spit or hot stones:** keep the meat turning.
- **Coals and clay:** wrap fish or small game in leaves and clay, then bury it in coals.
- **Pit cooking:** line a pit with hot stones, add food, cover.

## Hygiene
Wash hands before and after. Use separate tools for raw and cooked food. Never leave cooked food out more than two hours (one hour in heat).

## Do not eat
Liver of polar bear and some fish organs, or any meat from an animal that looks sick. Raw wild meat and raw eggs are risky.
