Living off the land is a calendar, not a single job.

## A year
- **Late winter:** order seeds, mend tools, plan the garden, hunt and fish. Stored food is lowest.
- **Spring:** plant, forage greens and morels, fish, tap maples where trees grow.
- **Summer:** weed and water, preserve fruit and garden surplus, cut hay, gather berries.
- **Fall:** harvest, nuts, deer season, preserve the meat, put up the wood.
- **Early winter:** slaughter, butcher, salt, smoke and eat well. Cut wood.

## The big idea
Everything you do in one season feeds the next. A family that plants, preserves and puts up wood in the warm months will eat through the cold ones.

## Keep records
Write down what you planted, harvested, preserved and used. The next year goes better.
