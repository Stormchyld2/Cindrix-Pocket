## Save your own seed
Choose the best plants, save their seed, and keep it dry and labelled.
go: Seed vault
button: Open the Seed vault

## Keep animals, if you can feed them
A small flock gives eggs, meat and manure. Plan the feed first.

## Cut wood for a year ahead
Always have next year's wood drying while you burn this year's.
check: lesson:living-off-the-land/03-the-long-view/01-firewood-and-fuel

## Mend and make everything
Handles, shoes, cloth, soap, candles and metal. Aim for a person who can do each.
go: training
button: Open the skills grid

## Teach
Pair every skill holder with a learner. A skill known by one person is a risk.
check: lesson:the-long-stay/03-resilience/02-records-teaching-and-moving-on

## Trade fairly
Trade skills and goods that keep, in the open and in daylight, and never trade away your last critical supply.
check: lesson:the-long-stay/02-people-and-trade/02-trade-and-community

## Keep a lasting watch
Keep a calm routine that does not tire people out, with a log and handovers.
check: lesson:the-long-stay/02-people-and-trade/03-security-over-time

## Look after the people
Watch for burnout, grief and quarrels. Settle them early and in the open.
check: lesson:the-long-stay/02-people-and-trade/01-leadership-and-morale

## Know when to move
Move only if staying is more dangerous than going. Plan the route, the supplies and the rally points first.

## Every season: review
List three things that worked, three that did not, and three changes.
every: 90
