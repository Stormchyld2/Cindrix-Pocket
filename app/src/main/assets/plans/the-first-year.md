## Days 1 to 3: safe, counted, decided
Make everyone safe. Count people, water, food, fuel and medicine. Agree who decides, and who does what.
go: What fails
button: Open What fails

## Week 1: a routine and a watch
Set a daily routine, a watch, a ration and a safe-water plan. Mark who can do what on the skills grid.
go: training
button: Open the skills grid

## Month 1: clean water and clean waste
Treat all drinking water and bury waste far from water. Wash hands every time.
check: lesson:the-long-stay/03-resilience/01-health-sanitation-and-teeth

## Month 1: count and replace
Use "What replaces it" to see what takes over each stored thing, and start the slow ones now.

button: See what replaces it

## Months 2 to 4: plant and preserve
Plant as soon as the season allows. Preserve every surplus.

## Months 2 to 4: learn the wild food near you
The wild-food course teaches plants, nuts, fungi, game and fish by season for West Virginia and Tennessee. Be certain of every food.

## Months 3 to 6: wood and warmth
Cut, split and stack a year of wood now so it is dry for winter. Sweep chimneys.
check: lesson:living-off-the-land/03-the-long-view/01-firewood-and-fuel

## Months 4 to 8: mend and make
Learn to sharpen, sew, mend shoes and make soap and candles.
go: Drills
button: Open Drills
check: lesson:trades-and-repair/01-edges-and-cloth/01-sharpening

## Fall: harvest, nuts, game
Gather nuts, hunt in season, and put the meat up. See the wild-food calendar on the Calendar screen.
check: lesson:appalachian-wild-food/05-the-year/01-calendar

## Winter: hold steady
Eat from what you stored. Hunt and fish, repair tools, plan the garden. Keep rest days and shared meals.

## Every season: review
Meet and list three things that worked, three that did not, and three changes.
every: 90
