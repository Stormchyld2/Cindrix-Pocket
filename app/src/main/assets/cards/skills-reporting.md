When you call for help or tell others what you have seen, give clear facts in the same order every time.
1. **Who:** how many people, and their ages and condition if it matters.
2. **What:** what happened or what you saw, in one sentence.
3. **Where:** address, landmarks or a grid reference. Say how you can be found.
4. **When:** the time it happened or when you first saw it.
5. **What you have done:** first aid, shutting off power, leaving the area.
6. **What you need:** an ambulance, fire service, police, rescue, supplies.
7. **For something you observed,** add: how many, what they were doing, which way they moved, what they carried, how long you watched.
8. **Stick to what you saw.** Say "it looked like" if unsure. Do not guess motives.
9. **Write it down** with the time.
10. **Repeat the address** at the end of a call and stay on the line until told to hang up.

## If you have less gear

- **No paper:** say it aloud to another person, who writes it, or scratch it on bark or a wall.
- **Short on air time:** give the six facts in the first 20 seconds.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Guessing, leaving out where you are, and talking too fast.

### Practice
Report what you see out the window in the six facts.

**Train:** Academy course "Communications".
