Signs soon after a food, sting or medicine: hives, swelling of lips, face, tongue or throat, trouble breathing or wheezing, vomiting, dizzy or faint, a feeling something is badly wrong.

1. **Use the person's epinephrine auto-injector right away**, exactly as printed on the device (usually into the outer thigh, through clothing is allowed). Do not wait for it to get worse.
2. If phones work, **call 911** even if they seem better. The reaction can come back.
3. Lay the person flat with legs raised. If breathing is hard, let them sit up. If they vomit, turn them on their side.
4. **Take away the cause:** scrape off a stinger, stop the food, rinse the mouth.
5. Stay with them. Start CPR if they stop breathing.

**Do not** stand or walk them, and do not rely on allergy pills in place of the injector.

Medicine amounts are not given here. Follow the person's written plan and the label on the device.

## If no help is coming

- **Second injector:** if the signs are not clearly better after 5 to 15 minutes, or they come back, use a second device if the person has one, following its label.
- **The reaction can return** hours later with no new exposure. Keep them resting and never alone for a full 24 hours, and keep any remaining injector within reach.
- **Allergy medicine by mouth** can ease hives and itching once they can swallow easily. Follow the label. It does not treat the dangerous part.
- **Check every 5 minutes** for the first hour, then every 30 minutes: breathing, voice, swelling of tongue and lips, pulse, color.
- **No injector at all:** lay them flat with legs up, keep them calm and still, and give allergy medicine by the label only if they can swallow. This is all you have, and it may not be enough. This is why a known allergy means carrying two injectors.

## Know more

### Mild reaction or dangerous one
Hives or itching alone, with normal breathing and no swelling in the mouth, is a mild reaction: watch closely. **Any** trouble breathing, a tight throat, a hoarse voice, swelling of the tongue, faintness, or repeated vomiting after a known trigger is the dangerous kind. Use the injector.

### Using an auto-injector
Read the picture steps on the device now, before you need them. Hold it in your fist, press it firmly into the middle of the outer thigh, and hold it there for the seconds the label says. It works through trousers. Never put your thumb over either end.

### Stings
Scrape the stinger out sideways with a fingernail or card. Squeezing it pushes more venom in. A cold cloth eases the swelling.

### Out-of-date injectors
An injector past its date is weaker but still far better than nothing if the liquid is clear. Use it.

### Have ready
Two in-date injectors for every person with a known severe allergy, a written allergy list for the whole group, and allergy tablets in the kit.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
