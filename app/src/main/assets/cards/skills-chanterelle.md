**Chanterelles have blunt ridges, not true gills. Check every one.**
1. Look in summer on the ground under hardwoods for orange-gold funnel-shaped mushrooms.
2. Look under the cap. It has **blunt, forking ridges** that run down the stem, not thin, sharp gills.
3. Check that the mushroom is not growing from wood.
4. Cook thoroughly and try a small amount first.

## If you have less gear

- **Unsure:** skip it.

## Know more

### Jack-o'-lantern look-alike
It grows in **clusters on wood**, has **thin, true gills**, and is poisonous. It can look like a chanterelle at a glance.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", mushroom lesson.

Source: North American Mycological Association; WVU Extension
