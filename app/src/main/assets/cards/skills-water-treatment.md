**Strain first.** If cloudy, pour through a clean cloth, coffee filter or paper towels, and let it settle.

1. **Boil (best).** Bring to a **rolling boil for 1 minute.** Above about 6,500 feet (2,000 m), boil for **3 minutes.** Let it cool covered.
2. **Filter.** A filter rated for bacteria and protozoa removes most germs. Check the label for viruses.
3. **Chemical.** Use **unscented household bleach** with 5 to 9 percent sodium hypochlorite and no added soap or scent. Follow the CDC table or label, shake well, and **wait 30 minutes.** Or use **purification tablets** exactly as on the package. Cold or cloudy water needs longer.
4. **Sunlight (last resort).** Clear plastic bottles in full sun for 6 hours (2 days if cloudy).
5. **Store** treated water in clean, covered containers and label with the date.
6. **Strong bleach smell:** let it stand with the lid off for an hour.

## If you have less gear

- **No pot:** heated stones dropped in a container can boil small amounts, or use bleach or sunlight.
- **No filter:** a cloth and a layer of sand and charcoal. See "Clear cloudy water before you treat it".
- **No bleach or tablets:** boil or use sunlight.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Not boiling long enough, using scented bleach, and skipping the strain.

### Practice
Treat a cup each way and note the time and effort.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".
