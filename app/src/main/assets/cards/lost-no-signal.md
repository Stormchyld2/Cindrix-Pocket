**S.T.O.P.**
- **Stop.** Sit down. Breathe. Do not wander.
- **Think.** When did you last know where you were?
- **Observe.** What do you have? What is the weather? How much light is left?
- **Plan.** Usually **stay put,** unless in danger. People who stay are found sooner.

**If phones work,** call 911 and keep the phone for later. A text may get through when a call will not.

1. Make yourself easy to find: stay in the open, tie bright cloth to a branch, lay out bright things.
2. **Signal in threes:** three whistle blasts, three fires, three flashes.
3. Get **warm and dry first.**

## If no help is coming

- **Tell people your plan** before you go, with a return time. Searchers start there.
- **Shelter and warmth come first** (rule of 3s: 3 hours without warmth, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food). See "Building a shelter".
- **Water:** drink treated water if you can. See "Finding water outdoors".
- **If you must move:** leave a note, mark your path, follow a stream or trail downhill, and move in daylight only. See "Lost: stop and think (S.T.O.P.)".
- **Map and compass:** if you have them, resect your position. See "Find where you are: resection with two landmarks".
- **At night:** stay put, huddle for warmth, and keep a fire going if you can.
- **Rescuers come by sound and light:** save the whistle for when you hear them.

## Know more

### Why people get lost
Leaving the trail, bad light, weather, and not turning back soon enough.

### Staying calm
Fear makes people wander. Eat, drink, and make a plan.

**Train:** Academy course "Land navigation".
