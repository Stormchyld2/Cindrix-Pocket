**Do not open the gate or door until you know who it is and why they are here.** If phones work and anyone is in danger, call 911.
1. **Stay inside or behind the gate.** Talk through the door, a window or the gate. Do not go out alone.
2. **Ask who it is and what they want.** Do not argue and do not tell them who else is here.
3. **Tell a second person.** Two people decide together. One watches while the other talks.
4. **Expected or known?** Check against your list of expected visitors. If not sure, they wait outside or come back.

## If no help is coming

- **Not welcome, or you do not feel safe:** say calmly that no one can help them here, and keep the gate closed. Note what you saw: time, vehicle, plate, number of people, what they said.
- **If they stay or try to get in,** keep everyone together, away from doors and windows, and use the camp alert. See "Alert or noise at the camp edge".
- **Someone forces the gate:** see "Someone has got inside".
- **Hungry or needy visitors:** decide ahead of time how the group handles them. A policy settled before a knock is better than one made in fear.
- **Write it down** in the Security log once everyone is safe. Set the site to **Elevated** for a while if you want extra checks.

## Know more

### Talk through a barrier
A gate or door stays between you and them. Keep your voice calm.

### What to note
Time, number of people, vehicle, plate, what they said and carried.

**Train:** Academy course "Home and site security".
