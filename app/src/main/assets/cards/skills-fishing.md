**Fish give a steady source of protein in streams and ponds.**
1. Get a licence and check the limits.
2. Use a hook, line and bait of worms, crickets or grasshoppers.
3. Fish deep, shaded pools and undercut banks, early or late.
4. Clean the fish at once, cook it to 145°F, and eat it the same day or dry or smoke it.

## If you have less gear

- **No hook:** a bent pin or a thorn works. A hand line works as well as a rod.

## Know more

### Advisories
Eat smaller fish and check state advisories on large predator fish.

### When
Spring through fall; catfish at night.

**Train:** The Academy course "Living off the land".

Source: WV DNR and TWRA fishing regulations
