In a crisis, water is the first limit on how long and how far you can go.
1. **Plan for at least 1 gallon (about 4 liters) per person per day** for drinking and basic hygiene. More in heat or hard work.
2. **Drink when thirsty and with each meal.** Drinking too little makes people sick and slow.
3. **Ration sweat, not water.** Rest in shade in the heat of the day, and travel in the cool morning and evening.
4. **Check your urine.** Pale yellow is good. Dark or very little means more water.
5. **Know where the next water is** before you leave the last one.
6. **Carry two ways to treat water** and containers for at least a day.
7. **Collect when you can:** fill every container at safe water, and collect rain with a clean tarp.
8. **Avoid salt water, urine and alcohol.**

## If you have less gear

- **Few containers:** use bags, pots, bottles and even a waterproof boot liner. Fill them all.
- **No treatment:** boil in any metal pot.
- **No source in sight:** see "Finding water outdoors".

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Setting out with too little, waiting until you are very thirsty, and not planning the route water to water.

### Practice
Plan a day's water for your family and measure how much you really use.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".
