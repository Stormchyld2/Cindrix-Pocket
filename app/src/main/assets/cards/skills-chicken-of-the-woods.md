**Eat only the tender edges of bright orange and yellow shelves from oak.**
1. Look in summer and fall on oak logs and trees for bright orange and yellow shelves.
2. Cut off the soft, young outer edges.
3. Avoid specimens growing on conifers.
4. Cook thoroughly. Never eat it raw.
5. Eat a small amount first. A few people react.

## If you have less gear

- **Unsure:** skip it.

## Know more

### Look-alikes
**Jack-o'-lantern** is orange, grows in clusters and has true gills, and is poisonous. Chicken of the woods has pores, not gills, and grows in shelves.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", mushroom lesson.

Source: North American Mycological Association; WVU Extension
