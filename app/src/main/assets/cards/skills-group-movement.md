A group moves at the pace of its slowest member and is only as safe as its tidiest habit.
1. **Put the slowest or youngest near the front,** so the group does not stretch out.
2. **Keep a leader and a last person** who know the route and keep each other in sight.
3. **Space people a few steps apart** so one hazard does not catch everyone.
4. **Rest about 10 minutes every hour,** and a longer break at midday. Drink and check feet.
5. **Fix hot spots** with tape or a pad at the first sign.
6. **Count heads** at every halt and junction.
7. **Share the load.** The strongest carry the most.
8. **Eat small amounts often.**
9. **Arrive with daylight left** to set up camp and water.
10. **Agree the stop signal** and what to do if someone falls behind: someone always stays with them.

## If you have less gear

- **No radios:** use whistle signals and hand signals. See "Silent hand signals for a group".
- **No leader:** pick one for the day and rotate.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
The line stretching out, no tail, and losing someone at a junction.

### Practice
A short group walk with a head count at every halt.

**Train:** Academy course "Mobility".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76) on movement and foot care, adapted for civilians.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
