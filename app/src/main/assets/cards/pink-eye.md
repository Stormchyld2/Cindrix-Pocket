1. **Do not touch or rub your eyes.**
2. Wash your hands with soap and water often. Help children do the same.
3. Do not share towels, bedding, glasses, makeup or eye drops.
4. **Stop wearing contact lenses** until an eye doctor says it is OK.

**Call 911** for a chemical in the eye (rinse with plenty of clean running water while you wait) or sudden loss of vision. **A newborn with any sign of pink eye needs a doctor right away.**

## If no help is coming

- **Most pink eye clears alone** in 1 to 2 weeks. Wipe crusts away with a clean, damp cloth, one wipe per cloth, from the inner corner outward.
- **A cool or warm damp cloth** over the closed eye eases the soreness.
- **Keep it from spreading:** own towel, wash pillowcases, and keep the sick person's hands clean.
- **Worse signs:** eye pain, light hurting the eyes, blurry vision, or very red eyes. See "Eye injury or chemical in the eye".
- **Antibiotic eye ointment** helps only if a clinician says it is bacterial.

## Know more

### Viral, bacterial, allergic
Viral and bacterial spread. Allergic does not. Allergic pink eye itches and comes with sneezing.

### Contact lenses
Wearing them with infection risks a deeper eye injury. Use glasses.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
