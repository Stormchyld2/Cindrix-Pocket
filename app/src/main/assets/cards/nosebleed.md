1. **Sit up and lean slightly forward.** Breathe through your mouth. Do not tip your head back.
2. **Pinch the soft part of the nose** firmly for **10 minutes.** Do not let go to check.
3. Catch the blood with a cloth. Do not stuff the nose.
4. Still bleeding? Pinch for another 10 minutes.

**If phones work, call 911** or go to the emergency room if it has not stopped after 30 minutes, the bleeding is heavy, it followed a hard blow to the head or face, or you feel faint.

## If no help is coming

- **Keep pinching:** a second and third round of 10 minutes stops most nosebleeds. A cold cloth on the nose and neck helps.
- **After it stops,** no blowing, picking, bending or heavy lifting for 24 hours. A dab of petroleum jelly inside the nostril keeps it moist.
- **If it will not stop:** pack the nostril lightly with a strip of clean gauze soaked in a nasal decongestant spray if you have one, leaving a tail out, and keep pinching. Remove it after 24 hours.
- **Swallowed blood** may make the person vomit. That is normal but check it was not heavy bleeding.
- **A heavy bleed after a head injury** is an emergency. See "Head injury or concussion".

## Know more

### Why they happen
Dry air, picking, colds, and a hit to the nose. Blood-thinning medicine makes them last longer.

### Preventing them
Humidify dry rooms and keep the inside of the nose moist.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
