A cold brings a runny nose, cough and sore throat, and sometimes a mild fever. It comes on slowly and gets better by itself. Antibiotics do not work on colds.

1. Rest and drink plenty of fluids.
2. Use salt-water nose drops or spray. For a small child, a rubber suction bulb clears a stuffy nose.
3. Breathe steam from a bowl of hot water, taking care not to scald.
4. Honey soothes a cough in anyone **1 year or older**. **Never give honey to a baby under 1.**
5. Do not give cough and cold medicine to children under 6.
6. Wash your hands often and cover coughs so it does not spread.

**It is an emergency** (call 911 if phones work) for trouble breathing, blue or gray lips, new confusion, or a person who cannot be woken.

## If no help is coming

- **A cold lasts 7 to 10 days** and needs only rest, fluids and time. The cough can linger for 3 weeks.
- **Watch for it turning into something else.** The warning pattern is getting better, then worse again with a new fever. That means a second infection in the chest, ears or sinuses.
- **Chest:** fast breathing, pain on breathing in, fever, coughing up yellow, green or rusty spit. See the card "Pneumonia warning signs".
- **Ears:** see the card "Earache or ear infection".
- **Sinuses:** pain and pressure in the face and upper teeth, worse bending forward, thick discharge for more than 10 days. Steam, warm cloths on the face, and salt-water rinses.
- **Keep the sick person resting and apart.** In a small group, one cold reaches everyone, and people who are ill cannot work or stand watch.

## Know more

### Salt-water rinse
A quarter teaspoon of salt in a cup of water that has been boiled and cooled. Drip or sniff it into each nostril, or gargle with it for a sore throat.

### Cough
A spoonful of honey, a warm drink, and sleeping propped up. A cough that brings up phlegm is doing a job: do not try to stop it completely.

### Babies
A baby with a blocked nose cannot feed. Put a few salt-water drops in each nostril and clear it with a suction bulb before feeds. A baby under 3 months with any fever needs care urgently.

### Cold or flu
A cold starts slowly in the nose and throat. Flu hits suddenly with fever, aching all over and exhaustion. See the card "The flu (influenza)".
