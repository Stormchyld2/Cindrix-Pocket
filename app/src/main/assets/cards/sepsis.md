**Sepsis is the body being overwhelmed by an infection. It can get worse in hours.**

Look for **an infection or recent injury** plus **one or more** of these:
- Fever, shivering or feeling very cold
- Confusion or being very sleepy or hard to wake
- Fast heartbeat or weak pulse
- Fast breathing or shortness of breath
- Clammy or sweaty skin
- Extreme pain or "the worst I have ever felt"

1. **Go to an emergency room now** if one can be reached, or call 911 if phones work. Say "I am worried about sepsis."
2. Keep the person warm and lying down.
3. Note when it started, the temperature, and any medicines taken.

## If no help is coming

- **Sepsis needs antibiotics and fluids into a vein.** If the group holds antibiotics supplied by a doctor for serious infection, this is exactly what they are for: start them at once, as directed, and do not stop early.
- **Fluids by mouth:** if the person is awake and can swallow, give rehydration drink steadily, a few mouthfuls every 5 minutes. See the card "Dehydration and diarrhea".
- **Deal with the source.** Open and wash out an infected wound, take out anything packed in it, let pus drain, and re-dress it. See the card "Signs a wound is infected".
- **Check every 30 minutes** and write it down: pulse, breaths per minute, how awake, skin, urine.
- **Getting worse:** more confused or sleepy, cold blotchy arms and legs, breathing over 30 a minute, no urine for 6 hours. See the card "Shock".
- **Getting better:** fever falling, pulse slowing, clearer thinking, passing urine.
- **Be honest:** without a hospital, many people with full sepsis do not survive. Catching infection early, before this point, is what saves lives.

## Know more

### Where it starts
Infected wounds and burns, chest infections, urine and kidney infections, a burst appendix or other belly infection, tooth abscesses, infection after childbirth or miscarriage, and skin infections in diabetics.

### Catching it early
Any infection that is spreading or not improving after 2 days needs action then, not later: cleaning and draining, rest, fluids, and antibiotics if you have them for that purpose.

### Who goes downhill fastest
Babies, old people, pregnant women and new mothers, diabetics, and anyone weak from hunger or another illness. In old people the only signs may be new confusion and fast breathing, with no fever at all.

### Low temperature
A very sick person can have a low temperature and cold skin in place of a fever. That is a worse sign, not a better one.
