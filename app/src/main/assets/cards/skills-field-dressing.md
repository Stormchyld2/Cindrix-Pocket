**Cool the meat below 40°F within hours. Check for signs of disease.**
1. Tag the animal as the law requires.
2. Wear gloves and check for signs of illness.
3. Roll it on its back, head uphill, and cut from breastbone to pelvis without puncturing the guts.
4. Free the lower intestine and remove the organs in one motion.
5. Drain and wipe the cavity.
6. Cool, skin and hang the meat in a cool, shaded, fly-proof place, or butcher it.

## If you have less gear

- **No hoist:** work on the ground, and cut it into quarters to carry it.

## Know more

### Disease
Chronic wasting disease has been found in both states. Follow state testing and carcass rules, and never eat an animal that looks sick.

### Cooking
Ground game to 160°F, whole cuts to 145°F with a rest, poultry to 165°F.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", field dressing lesson.

Source: WV Division of Natural Resources and Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency; USDA FSIS safe temperatures
