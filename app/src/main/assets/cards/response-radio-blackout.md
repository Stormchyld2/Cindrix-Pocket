**Someone must always be able to hear emergency calls while anyone is away. If phones work and someone may be hurt, call 911.**

**If the base radio fails:**
1. **Switch to a working spare** and take the listening position again.
2. **Check power:** battery, charging cable, connections.
3. **Tell everyone** which radio is now the base.

**If a patrol cannot reach base:**
1. **Check** volume, battery, channel and privacy-code setting.
2. **Move to a better spot** and try again, then try the back-up channel.

## If no help is coming

- **Use the agreed back-up check-in:** a fixed time and place to meet, or runners in pairs, until radio is back.
- **Go back to the meeting place** only when it is safe. Do not cross bad terrain, weather or darkness just to get radio back.
- **If someone is hurt or stranded,** signal for help first. Safety comes before the routine return.
- **A missed check-in alone is not proof of danger.** See "Missed check-in or radio contact lost".
- **Write down** what failed and when, then fix it. Keep spare radios, batteries and a charger.

## Know more

### Why a spare
One radio is one failure.

### Rules of thumb
Higher ground, away from metal, and line of sight improve reception.

**Train:** Academy course "Communications".
