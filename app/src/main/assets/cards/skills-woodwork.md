**Clamp the work, keep tools sharp, and saw on the waste side of the line.**
1. Mark with a pencil and a square.
2. Saw on the waste side of the line with long light strokes.
3. Chisel away from you, with your other hand behind the blade.
4. Plane with the grain.
5. Join with pegs, glue or screws, and test.
6. Use seasoned hardwood such as hickory or ash for handles.

## If you have less gear

- **No saw:** a hatchet and a knife shape wood slowly.

## Know more

### Safety
Wear safety glasses and work in good light.

### Practice
The drill "Make a tool handle or a stool".

**Train:** The Academy course "Trades and repair".

Source: Extension and craft guidance; OSHA hand tool safety
