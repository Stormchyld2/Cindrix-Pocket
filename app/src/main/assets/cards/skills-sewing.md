**Learn the backstitch and a patch, and your clothes last for years.**
1. Thread a needle and tie a knot.
2. Use a **running stitch** for quick seams and a **backstitch** for strength.
3. Cut a patch larger than the hole and fold its edges under.
4. Pin it and sew around the edge with small, even stitches.
5. Pull to test the repair.

## If you have less gear

- **No thread:** unravel a seam from old cloth, or use dental floss.

## Know more

### Kit
Needles, heavy thread, patches, thimble and safety pins.

### Practice
The drill "Sew a patch and a seam".

**Train:** The Academy course "Trades and repair".
