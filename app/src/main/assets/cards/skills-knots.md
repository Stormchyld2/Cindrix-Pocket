1. **Square (reef) knot:** joins two ropes of the same size. Right over left and under, then left over right and under. **Not safe for anything critical.**
2. **Bowline:** a loop that will not slip. Make a small loop in the standing end (the "rabbit hole"), bring the working end up through it (the rabbit comes out), around behind the tree (standing end), and back down the hole. Pull tight.
3. **Clove hitch:** a fast way to tie a rope to a post or a pole. Two turns around, crossing, with the end tucked under the last turn.
4. **Taut-line hitch:** an adjustable loop for tarp or tent lines. Wrap twice inside the loop and once outside, then slide to tighten.
5. **Two half hitches:** a quick way to fasten a rope to a ring or tree. Two simple knots one after the other on the standing end.

**Tips:** learn them now with rope in your hands. Leave a long tail. Check each knot before you trust it. **Never rely on a knot you have not tested** for weight.

## If you have less gear

- **No rope:** twist strips of cloth, inner bark, vines or long grass into cord. Test it.
- **Paracord** has 7 inner strands you can pull out for sewing and fishing line.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Too short a tail, a knot that is not dressed (twisted), and a square knot used to hold weight.

### Practice
Tie each one with your eyes closed, then in the dark and with cold hands.

**Train:** Academy course "Basic building and repair".
