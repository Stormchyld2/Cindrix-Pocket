**Place traps on fresh trails, check them daily, and keep them away from people and pets.**
1. Check the state rules first: licences, seasons and methods.
2. Look for fresh trails, droppings and chewed plants.
3. Wear gloves and hide scent.
4. Set a box trap or a wire snare on the trail.
5. Check every day and dispatch the animal quickly.

## If you have less gear

- **No wire:** use strong cord, though it holds poorly.

## Know more

### Safety
Keep away from paths where people, children or pets walk.

### Deadfalls
They are dangerous to set. Practise with a light weight first.

**Train:** The Academy course "Living off the land".

Source: State trapping regulations; US Army FM 21-76
