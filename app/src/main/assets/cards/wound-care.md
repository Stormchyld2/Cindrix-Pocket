1. Wash your hands. Stop bleeding with firm pressure on clean cloth.
2. **Rinse the wound well** with plenty of clean water. Wash around it with soap. Pick out dirt with clean tweezers.
3. Cover with a clean bandage. Change it every day or when it is wet or dirty.
4. **Animal bite:** wash with soap and water for a full 5 minutes. Note the animal.
5. **See a doctor if one can be reached** for a deep or gaping wound, one that will not stop bleeding, an animal bite, or signs of infection.

**Do not** blow on a wound, lick it, or close a dirty one.

## If no help is coming

- **Cleaning is what prevents infection, and you can do it as well as a clinic.** Use water safe enough to drink, at least a quart for a small wound and several for a large or dirty one, squirted with force from a syringe or a bag with a pinhole.
- **Close or leave open:** close only a clean, straight cut less than 8 hours old, with tape strips pulling the edges just together. Leave open and pack loosely any wound that is dirty, ragged, deep, a puncture, a bite, or older than 8 hours. An open wound heals slower but far more safely.
- **Dress it:** non-stick layer, padding, bandage. Change daily, washing each time.
- **Rest and raise** the wounded part for the first 2 days.
- **Check daily** for infection. See the card "Signs a wound is infected".
- **Tetanus:** a deep or dirty wound needs a booster when care can be reached, if the last was more than 5 years ago.

## Know more

### Closing a clean cut without stitches
Dry the skin. Stick strips of tape across the cut, starting at the middle, each pulling the edges just to touching, with gaps between strips for drainage. On the scalp, twist strands of hair from each side together across the cut and tie them. Keep it dry for 2 days.

### Scrapes
Scrub out every bit of grit, even though it hurts, or it tattoos the skin and breeds infection. Cover with a non-stick dressing and keep it moist with petroleum jelly or honey.

### Punctures
A nail, thorn or tooth pushes dirt deep and seals over. Soak it in warm clean water, encourage it to bleed a little, do not close it, and watch it closely for 5 days.

### Animal bites and rabies
Wash for a full 5 minutes with soap and running water. This alone removes much of the risk. Do not close the wound. A bite from a bat, raccoon, skunk, fox, or any animal acting strangely carries rabies risk, and rabies is fatal once it shows. It is prevented by shots given soon after the bite, which is a strong reason to travel to care.

### Human bites and fist-on-teeth cuts
These infect very easily, especially over the knuckles. Wash hard, leave open, splint the hand, and watch.

### Have ready
Soap, a large syringe, clean stored water, tweezers, non-stick dressings, gauze, tape strips, bandages, petroleum jelly and gloves.

**Train:** Academy course "Casualty care in a crisis".
