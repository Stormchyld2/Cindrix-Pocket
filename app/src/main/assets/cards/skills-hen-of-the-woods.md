**Eat only mushrooms with no look-alike you cannot rule out.**
1. **Hen of the woods (maitake):** grey-brown frilly clusters at the base of oaks in fall. Cook.
2. **Giant puffball:** a white ball that is solid white inside, like marshmallow. Cut it open: any shape or colour inside means do not eat.
3. Slice, cook and eat a small amount first.

## If you have less gear

- **Unsure:** skip.

## Know more

### Look-alikes
Young **Amanita** mushrooms look like puffballs, but cutting one open shows the outline of a cap and stem. This has killed people. Cut every puffball open.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", mushroom lesson.

Source: North American Mycological Association; WVU Extension
