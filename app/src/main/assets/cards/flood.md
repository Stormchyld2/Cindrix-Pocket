**Turn around, don't drown.** Six inches of moving water can knock you down. One foot can carry a car away.
1. **Leave at once** if told to. Never drive around barricades.
2. Move to higher ground or the top floor. Do not climb into a closed attic.
3. **Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.** It hides holes and live wires and carries sewage.
4. **Car stalls in rising water:** get out and move to higher ground. If trapped, climb onto the roof. **If phones work, call 911.**

## If no help is coming

- **Go early.** Move before roads flood. Take the go-bag, water, medicines and papers.
- **Cannot leave:** go to the highest floor, take water, food, a light, a radio, and a way to signal from a window or roof. Bring an axe or saw if the attic is your last refuge.
- **Rescue:** throw, do not go. Throw a rope or float to a person in water. See "Drowning or water rescue".
- **After:** go home only when it is safe. Turn off power from a dry place, run a generator outdoors only, and throw away food that touched floodwater.
- **Water is not safe** until tested. See "Making water safe: boil, filter, treat".
- **Mold and mud:** wear boots, gloves and a mask. Dry the house fast.

## Know more

### How it comes
Flash floods rise in minutes from rain upstream you cannot see. Dry riverbeds and low roads are death traps.

### Know your high ground
Pick two routes and a meeting point that stays dry.

**Train:** Academy course "Home damage control".
