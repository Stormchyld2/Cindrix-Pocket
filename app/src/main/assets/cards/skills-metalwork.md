**Protect your eyes. Never heat or weld galvanised metal.**
1. Wear eye protection, leather gloves, boots and cotton or wool clothes.
2. Check the metal. Do not heat galvanised, painted or unknown metal.
3. Cut with a hacksaw, file, drill and join with bolts or rivets.
4. If you forge, heat only mild steel to orange, hammer and quench.
5. Clear flammable material and keep water or sand close.

## If you have less gear

- **No power:** use cold work only: a hacksaw, files, a vise and bolts.

## Know more

### Fumes
Zinc fumes from galvanised metal cause metal fume fever. Work outdoors.
