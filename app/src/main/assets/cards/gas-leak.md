**Signs:** a smell like rotten eggs, a hissing sound, dead plants near a pipe, bubbles in standing water.

1. **Do not** use switches, lighters, matches, phones, doorbells or anything that could spark. Do not start a car in the garage.
2. **Get everyone out** at once. Take pets. Leave doors open if it is quick.
3. Go well away from the building, upwind.
4. **Call 911 and the gas company from outside** if a phone works.
5. Do not go back in until the gas company says it is safe.

## If no help is coming

- **Shut off the gas** at the meter only if you can do it quickly and safely: turn the valve a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe. Use a wrench.
- **Air it out:** open doors and windows on your way out, then stay out. Wait at least an hour.
- **No sparks:** no switches, no flames, no vehicle. Use a flashlight carried in from outside.
- **Propane:** heavy, it settles low. Stay uphill and upwind. Shut the tank valve.
- **Dizzy or sick** near the leak: get to fresh air and sit up. See "Carbon monoxide".
- **After an earthquake or flood** assume the gas lines are damaged. See "Earthquake".
- **Do not relight** pilot lights. Have a professional check first.

## Know more

### Rotten egg smell
Gas has no smell. A sulfur odor is added so it can be noticed.

### Alarms
A natural gas alarm and a CO alarm help.

**Train:** Academy course "Home damage control".

Source: Gas utility and PHMSA safety guidance.
