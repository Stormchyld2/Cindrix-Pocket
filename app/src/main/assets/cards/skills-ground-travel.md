1. **Plan the route** before you leave. Tell someone your route, your group and when you expect to be back.
2. **Move at the pace of the slowest** person. Rest 10 minutes every hour.
3. **Keep together.** Buddy system. Count heads at every stop.
4. **Keep a leader and a last person** who watches the group.
5. **Heavy items close to the back and center** of the pack.
6. **Drink and eat** at every rest. Fix hot spots on feet at once.
7. **Watch the light.** Camp with **2 hours of daylight left.**
8. **Mark your trail** if lost, and turn back if the weather turns.
9. **Check each other** for cold, heat, exhaustion and injury.

## If you have less gear

- **No pack:** a rolled blanket, a pillowcase or a sack with straps, a belt for a load.
- **No boots:** wrap feet in layers of cloth and bags, and rest often.
- **No map:** follow roads, streams and rail lines and note landmarks.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Going too fast, too far, too late in the day. Starting a trip with sore feet. Not drinking.

### Practice
Walk a day with the full load to find the hot spots before they matter.

**Train:** Academy course "Mobility".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased.
