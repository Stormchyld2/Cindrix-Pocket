**Eat only ripe, cooked elderberries. Raw berries, leaves and stems make people sick.**
1. Find a shrub with large flat clusters of tiny dark purple berries in late summer.
2. Strip ripe berries from the stems and pick out all stems and green berries.
3. Cook them for at least 15 minutes into syrup, jam or juice.
4. Never eat the leaves, bark, roots or stems.

## If you have less gear

- **No cooking fire:** leave them.
- **Not sure:** skip.

## Know more

### Look-alikes
**Water hemlock** and **pokeweed** can look similar from a distance. Elder has opposite, toothed compound leaves and woody stems; poke has red stems and single leaves.

### When
August to September.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".

Source: WVU Extension and University of Tennessee Extension foraging guides; Peterson Field Guide to Edible Wild Plants
