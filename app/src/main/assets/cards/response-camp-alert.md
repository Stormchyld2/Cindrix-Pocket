**An alert means "go and verify safely", not "someone is attacking". Check in pairs, and keep one person in camp. If phones work and anyone is in danger, call 911.**
1. **Wake or call the watch lead.** Do not go and look alone.
2. **Watch first.** From a safe place, listen and look toward the alert for a few minutes.
3. **Send a pair, not one,** with a radio, a light and a stopping point they will not pass. A third stays at camp.
4. **Radio in** when you leave, at the stopping point, and when you are back.

## If no help is coming

- **Animal or false alarm?** Reset the alert, note it, and look at the cause (battery, line, wind).
- **A person, a vehicle, or you cannot tell?** Keep your distance, do not confront. Pull back to camp, keep everyone together, and move the site to **Elevated**.
- **A late check-in** is a reason to verify, not to assume harm. See "Missed check-in or radio contact lost".
- **If anyone is threatened or hurt,** go to "Someone has got inside" or "Active shooter or attacker".
- **Write it down** in the Security log: time, which alert, who checked, what was found.
- **Reset after a few hours** and check the cause so false alarms fall.

## Know more

### The pairs rule
Pairs go out, one stays in camp, and an alert starts a check, not an assumption.

### Common false alarms
Wind, animals, a loose line, a flat battery, a branch.

**Train:** Academy course "Home and site security".
