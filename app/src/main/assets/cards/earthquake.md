**During: Drop, Cover, Hold On.**
1. **Drop** to hands and knees. **Cover** head and neck with your arms under a sturdy table. **Hold on** until the shaking stops.
2. **Indoors:** stay inside. Do not run outside. Do not stand in a doorway.
3. **In bed:** stay there and cover your head with a pillow.
4. **Outdoors:** stay in the open, away from buildings, trees and power lines.
5. **In a car:** pull over in the open and stay inside.
6. **After:** expect aftershocks, check for injuries, and **if phones work, use texts, not calls.** Call 911 for life-threatening injuries.
7. **Near the coast, go inland or uphill at once** after the shaking stops. See "Tsunami".

## If no help is coming

- **Check everyone** for injuries, starting with bleeding and breathing. See "Severe bleeding".
- **Smell gas?** Open windows, leave, and do not use switches or flames. See "Gas leak". Shut off the gas at the meter only if you must.
- **Damaged building:** get out and stay out. Do not go back for things.
- **Trapped:** cover your mouth, tap on a pipe or wall, or use a whistle. Do not shout. It saves air.
- **Water and power:** assume pipes and wires are damaged. Shut off water if lines are broken. Treat tap water as unsafe.
- **Rescue:** only trained people should enter rubble. Mark with a clear sign where you left an injured person.
- **Cleanup:** boots, heavy gloves, long sleeves, and a mask for dust.

## Know more

### Fix it before
Strap water heaters and tall furniture. Put heavy items low. Keep shoes and a flashlight by the bed.

### Aftershocks
They can come for days. Each one can bring down damaged walls.

**Train:** Academy course "Earthquake preparedness".
