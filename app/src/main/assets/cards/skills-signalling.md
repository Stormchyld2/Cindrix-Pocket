**Rule of three:** three of anything is a call for help. Three whistle blasts, three fires in a triangle, three flashes of light, three shots.
1. **Whistle:** three sharp blasts, a pause, and repeat. It carries farther than your voice.
2. **Mirror or shiny object:** flash it toward aircraft or people. Practice with the sun behind you.
3. **Fire and smoke:** three fires in a line or triangle, 100 feet apart. At night, bright flame. In daylight, add green leaves for thick white smoke.
4. **Ground signals,** large and in contrast with the ground, at least 10 feet (3 m) long, made of rocks, logs or trampled snow:
   - **V:** we need help
   - **X:** we need medical help
   - **Arrow:** we went this way
   - **SOS:** written large
5. **Bright cloth** hung high in an open space.
6. **Phone:** save battery. Text often works when calls do not.
7. **Stay put** and in the open.

## If you have less gear

- **No whistle:** shout in threes, or bang on a tin or log.
- **No mirror:** a phone screen, a CD, or foil.
- **No fuel:** burn damp wood and tires if safe, or lay out bright cloth.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Signals too small, moving away from the signal, and giving up at night.

### Practice
Try each signal and see how far it can be seen or heard.

**Train:** Academy course "First 72 hours".
