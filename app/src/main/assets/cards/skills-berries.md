**Pick only berries you can name, and leave the rest.**
1. **Blackberry and raspberry:** thorny canes, compound leaves, berries that pull off leaving a hollow core.
2. **Blueberry and huckleberry:** low shrubs with round blue berries that have a crown.
3. **Wild strawberry** and **mulberry** are also safe when ripe.
4. Wash, and eat a small amount first.
5. Dry or cook the surplus.

## If you have less gear

- **No bucket:** use a hat or a shirt as a basket.
- **Unsure:** skip it.

## Know more

### Look-alikes and poisons
**Pokeberry** (dark berries on red stems), **Virginia creeper** (five leaflets), **moonseed** (looks like wild grape but one crescent seed and no tendrils), **baneberry** (white or red) and **nightshades** are poisonous. Skip any berry you cannot name.

### When
June to September.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".

Source: WVU Extension and University of Tennessee Extension foraging guides; Peterson Field Guide to Edible Wild Plants
