**Start the search early. Do not wait until dark. If phones work and the person is hurt, a child, or in danger, call 911.**
1. **Note the time** and write down the last place and time they were seen, what they wore, and what they took.
2. **Try to reach them:** radio, phone, a call out loud, the whistle (three blasts). Check the usual places first.
3. **Look at the plan they gave.** Where were they going, and when were they due back?
4. **Search in pairs.** Never send one searcher alone. Leave someone at camp with the radio and a light.

## If no help is coming

- **Agree a turn-back time** and signals for "found" and "need help" before anyone leaves.
- **Mark where you have looked.** Follow trails, streams and roads that lead back. Do not widen the search so far that a second person is lost.
- **Night coming:** keep a fire and a light at camp, and a whistle or radio check each hour.
- **A child or an injured person:** search sooner and closer, and prepare to treat for cold. See "Hypothermia and frostbite".
- **The missing person:** use "Lost with no phone signal": stop, stay put, make yourself easy to find, signal in threes.
- **When found:** treat injuries, warm, water, and rest. Then write it up.

## Know more

### Where people go
Along trails, downhill, and following water. Children often hide.

### Plans before trips
Always leave a route and a return time with someone.

**Train:** Academy course "Land navigation".
