**Keep Clean, avoid Overheating, wear Loose layers, keep Dry.**
1. **Layers:** a wicking layer next to skin, an insulating layer (fleece or wool), and a windproof, waterproof outer layer. No cotton next to the skin.
2. **Do not sweat.** Open vents, take a layer off and put it back when you stop.
3. **Cover your head, neck, hands and feet.**
4. **Keep dry.** Change wet socks and gloves promptly.
5. **Eat and drink regularly.** Warm sweet drinks help.
6. **Get out of the wind** and **off the ground.**
7. **Move to warm up,** but do not exhaust yourself.
8. **Watch each other** for shivering that will not stop, slurred speech, stumbling, or going quiet. See "Hypothermia and frostbite".

## If you have less gear

- **No sleeping pad:** a thick layer of dry leaves, pine boughs or a pack under you. The ground steals more heat than the air.
- **No jacket:** stuff dry leaves or grass between layers, in a bag or loose shirt.
- **No stove:** a fire, or a hot water bottle in the sleeping bag.
- **Share heat:** sleep close together in a pile, with blankets over all.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Cotton, sweating, tight boots and gloves, not eating, and sleeping on bare ground.

### Practice
Spend a cold night in the yard and learn what keeps you warm.

### Winter shelter
A shelter just big enough for your body is warmest. See "Building a shelter".

**Train:** Academy course "Cold-weather living".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC winter weather guidance.
