1. **Do not cross if you do not have to.** Look for a bridge or a wide shallow stretch, or wait for the water to drop.
2. **Look upstream and downstream.** Choose a wide, shallow section, not a narrow chute, a bend or a place above rapids or a waterfall.
3. **Fast water is dangerous at knee depth.** Six inches (15 cm) can knock a person down.
4. **Unbuckle hip and chest straps** so you can shed the pack if you fall.
5. **Keep your boots on** and face **upstream,** using a strong stick as a third point of contact.
6. **Cross at an angle** downstream with the current.
7. **In a group,** link arms, strongest at the upstream end.
8. **If swept away:** lie on your back, feet pointing downstream, and swim toward shore at an angle.
9. **Never cross flooded roads.** **Turn around, don't drown.**

## If you have less gear

- **No pole:** a sturdy branch. Or cross as a line holding each other.
- **No rope:** a line made of belts and straps for a hand line. Never tie it around a person.
- **Cold water:** have dry clothes and a fire ready on the far side.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Crossing at a bend, keeping the pack buckled, and crossing at the end of the day.

### Practice
Practice with a pole in a slow, shallow stream, not in a flood.

**Train:** Academy course "Mobility".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; National Weather Service flood safety guidance.
