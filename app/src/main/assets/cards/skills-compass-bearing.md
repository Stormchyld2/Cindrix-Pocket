1. **Hold the compass flat,** level, away from metal, phones and vehicles.
2. **Magnetic north** is where the needle points. True north differs by the **declination** printed on your map's margin. Add or subtract it as the map says.
3. **To follow a direction:** turn the dial until the direction you want lines up with the travel arrow. Turn your body until the red end of the needle sits in the orienting arrow. Walk the way the travel arrow points.
4. **Pick a landmark** on the line, walk to it, then pick the next. Do not stare at the compass while walking.
5. **Back bearing:** add or subtract 180 degrees. If you went on 70, go back on 250.
6. **Around an obstacle:** take 90-degree turns and count paces, then turn back with the same count.

## If you have less gear

- **No compass:** see "Finding direction without a compass".
- **Cheap compass without a dial:** use the needle and a map. See "Orient a map and keep it oriented".
- **A phone compass** works but drains power and drifts near metal.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Holding it near a knife, phone or vehicle. Forgetting declination. Following the needle instead of a landmark.

### Practice
Walk a known bearing across a field and see how far off you land.

**Train:** Academy course "Land navigation".
