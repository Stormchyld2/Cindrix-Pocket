**Acorns are one of the best wild foods, and they must be leached.**
1. Gather acorns in fall. **White oak** (round-lobed leaves) are sweeter than **red oak** (pointed lobes).
2. Reject any with holes or mould.
3. Shell and chop them.
4. Soak in cold water, changing it twice a day, until the water runs clear and the nut tastes mild (1 to 3 days for white oak, longer for red).
5. Dry and grind to flour for mush or flatbread.

## If you have less gear

- **No grinder:** pound with a flat stone.
- **No time:** boil in several changes of water.

## Know more

### Warning
Raw acorns in quantity cause stomach pain.

### Storing
Keep dry in a cool, rodent-proof place.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".

Source: WVU Extension and University of Tennessee Extension foraging guides; Peterson Field Guide to Edible Wild Plants
