**Lye burns. Wear goggles and gloves, and add lye to water, never water to lye.**
1. Wear goggles, gloves and long sleeves, and clear children and pets away.
2. Weigh fat and lye using a lye calculator.
3. Add lye to water slowly, stir, and let it cool.
4. Pour it into warm fat, stir to trace, pour into a mould, and cure for 4 to 6 weeks.
5. For light, make candles by dipping a cotton wick, or an oil lamp with vegetable oil and a wick. Never use gasoline.
6. Never leave a flame unattended, and keep water or sand nearby.

## If you have less gear

- **No lye:** soapwort or yucca gives a mild lather.

## Know more

### If lye splashes
Rinse skin with water for 15 minutes. For eyes, rinse and get help at once.

### Practice
The drills "Make a small batch of soap" and "Make a candle and a safe oil lamp".

**Train:** The Academy course "Trades and repair".
