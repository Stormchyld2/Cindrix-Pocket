1. Wash your hands and the area with soap and water.
2. If the end sticks out, pull it out with clean tweezers the way it went in.
3. Squeeze gently to bleed it a little, wash again, and cover.

**See a doctor** for a splinter that is deep or in the eye, or glass or metal you cannot remove. If phones work, call 911 for an eye injury.

## If no help is coming

- **Under the skin:** clean a needle with alcohol or a flame, lift the skin over the end, and ease the splinter out with tweezers.
- **Tape trick:** for tiny spines, press tape on and peel it off.
- **Cannot reach it:** a warm soak twice a day often pushes it to the surface.
- **Do not dig deep.** Leave a deep one in, cover it, and watch for infection. See "Signs a wound is infected".
- **Check tetanus** status and see a clinic when you can.

## Know more

### Why remove them
Wood and thorn carry dirt and germs and cause infection if left.

### Glass or metal
Often not worth digging for. Clean and cover unless it is deep or near a joint.
