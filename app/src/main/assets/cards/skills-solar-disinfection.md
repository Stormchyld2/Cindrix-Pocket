Sunlight can make clear water safer to drink. It is slow and only works when the water is clear. If you can boil water, boil it instead.
1. **Use clear plastic bottles** (PET drink bottles), not colored or glass. Two liters or smaller is best. Remove the labels.
2. **Pre-filter** cloudy water through a clean cloth until you can read print through the bottle.
3. **Fill the bottle about three quarters,** cap it and shake for 20 seconds, then fill to the top. This adds oxygen.
4. **Lay the bottles on their sides** in full sun, on a reflective or dark surface such as a metal sheet or a roof.
5. **Leave them at least 6 hours** on a clear day, or **two full days** if mostly cloudy. In rain or heavy cloud, use another method.
6. **Drink from the bottle** or pour into clean cups.
7. **Keep treated bottles covered and clean.** Old, scratched bottles work less well.
8. **It does not remove chemicals.** Do not drink water that may hold fuel or chemicals.

## If you have less gear

- **No clear bottle:** a clear plastic bag laid in the sun works for small amounts.
- **No sun:** boil or use bleach. See "Making water safe: boil, filter, treat".
- **Cloudy water:** pre-filter first. See "Clear cloudy water before you treat it".

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Cloudy water, colored bottles, and too little time in the sun.

### Practice
Treat a few bottles and keep a record of the days of the week and the sun.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".

Source: WHO and Eawag (Swiss Federal Institute of Aquatic Science) guidance on solar water disinfection, paraphrased.
