1. **Bucket toilet:** line a 5-gallon bucket (or the toilet bowl) with a heavy garbage bag. Add a seat if you have one.
2. After each use, **cover the waste with** cat litter, sawdust, ash or dry soil.
3. Tie the bag when half full. Keep it in a lidded container away from where you live, cook and store water.
4. **Wash hands** with soap and clean water for 20 seconds. Without water, use hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol.
5. Keep waste away from food and drinking water.

If someone gets very sick from dirty water and phones work, call 911.

## If no help is coming

- **No toilet or bags:** dig a hole at least 200 feet from water, 6 to 8 inches deep, and cover it. Move the spot each time for a group.
- **A trench latrine** for a group: a long narrow trench, covered each day, with a hand-washing station beside it.
- **Hand washing station:** a jug with a small hole or a tap, soap, and a place to catch the water. Place it at the latrine and the kitchen.
- **Never** use waste on food gardens without long composting. Bury it.
- **Illness:** diarrhea spreads fast without sanitation. See "Dehydration and diarrhea" and "Stop a contagious illness from spreading".
- **Waste storage:** a few days of bags, ready lids, lime or ash.

## Know more

### Why it matters
In long emergencies, bad sanitation kills more people than anything else.

### Odor and flies
Cover each use. Keep lids on. Keep the area away from the kitchen.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".

Source: CDC and Ready.gov sanitation guidance.
