1. Rest the stomach for 15 to 30 minutes. Then small sips of clear fluid: water or an oral rehydration drink.
2. A sip every few minutes. A child can have a teaspoon at a time.
3. After several hours with no vomiting, offer plain foods: rice, bread, bananas, toast.
4. Wash hands and clean surfaces. Keep the sick person's towels and cups separate.
5. Breastfed babies should keep nursing, in small amounts more often.

**If phones work, call 911 or get care** for blood in vomit, green vomit, severe belly pain, a stiff neck, vomiting after a head injury, or signs of dehydration (no urine for 8 hours, dry mouth, very sleepy).

## If no help is coming

- **Dehydration is the danger.** See "Dehydration and diarrhea". Make a rehydration drink: a liter of clean water with 6 level teaspoons of sugar and half a level teaspoon of salt.
- **Babies and small children dehydrate fast.** Give a teaspoon every minute or two, and keep it up through the night.
- **Check urine:** pale and regular is good. None in 8 hours, or a sunken look, needs care at once.
- **Poison?** If something swallowed could be the cause, see "Poisoning or swallowed something".
- **Belly pain that is worse on the right side, or hard and swollen,** see "Severe belly pain".
- **Stop spread:** one bucket, bleach or hot soapy cleaning, and hand washing. See "Stop a contagious illness from spreading".

## Know more

### Common causes
Stomach viruses, food poisoning, and dirty water. Check water first. See "Making water safe: boil, filter, treat".

### Foods to avoid at first
Greasy, sugary or spicy foods, milk, and fizzy drinks.
