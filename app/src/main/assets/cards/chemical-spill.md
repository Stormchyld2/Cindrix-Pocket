If told to **shelter in place**, or you see or smell a chemical release:
1. **Go indoors now.** Bring pets in. Close and lock doors and windows.
2. Turn off fans, air conditioning and heat that pull in outside air. Close fireplace dampers.
3. Go to an **interior room with few windows,** above ground if you can. Take the radio, phone and emergency kit.
4. **If phones work, call 911** or Poison Help (1-800-222-1222) for anyone exposed.
5. Stay tuned for instructions from officials.

**If you were outside or touched it:** take off outer clothing without pulling it over the head (cut it off), bag it, wash all skin with soap and water, and rinse eyes for 15 minutes if they sting.

## If no help is coming

- **No alerts?** Trust your eyes and nose: a cloud, a smell, dying plants or animals, a tanker crash. Go upwind, uphill and away, at right angles to the wind.
- **Sealing a room** works only for a few hours. Use plastic sheeting and tape around windows, doors and vents. Open windows after the cloud has passed.
- **Evacuate** upwind with windows up and vents closed.
- **Decontaminate:** removing clothes takes off most of the harm. Wash skin and hair. Bag the clothes and keep them outside.
- **Burning, coughing or trouble breathing:** move to clean air, sit up, and rinse eyes and skin. See "Poisoning or swallowed something".
- **Do not go back** until the air has been cleared by someone who can test it.

## Know more

### Which way to go
Wind carries vapors. Stand with the wind at your back and move across it, then away.

### Chlorine vs ammonia
Chlorine is heavy and sinks. Go up. Ammonia rises. Go low and upwind.

### Smoke clouds
The same steps apply. Wet cloth over the nose helps a little.

**Train:** Academy course "Operator CBRN and hazmat protection".
