1. **Stop the bleeding first.** Press firmly on the stump with clean cloth. For an arm or leg that will not stop bleeding, put on a tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound and write down the time.
2. If phones work, **call 911.**
3. Lay the person down, raise the injured part, and keep them warm.
4. Cover the wound with a clean dressing and bandage it firmly.
5. **The cut-off part:** rinse off dirt gently with clean water, wrap it in clean damp cloth, seal it in a plastic bag, and set the bag on ice or in cold water. **Never put the part straight onto ice.** Send it with the person.
6. **A part still hanging on:** put it back in line, cover it, splint it, and do not cut it free.

**Do not** scrub the wound, put the part in water, or give food or drink if a hospital can be reached.

## If no help is coming

- **A hospital within about 6 hours for a hand or limb, or a day for a finger kept cold:** go. Rejoining is only possible there.
- **No hospital:** the part cannot be saved. Put your effort into the person.
- **Tourniquet:** follow the card "Severe bleeding" for when it can be eased and when it must stay.
- **Dress the stump:** rinse with plenty of clean drinking water, cover with a non-stick dressing, pad well, and bandage firmly from the end upward. Keep it raised for the first 2 days.
- **Change the dressing daily** and watch for infection: spreading redness, heat, pus, bad smell, fever. See the card "Signs a wound is infected".
- **Shock** is likely after a large amputation. See the card "Shock".

## Know more

### Fingertips
A fingertip cut off beyond the last joint usually heals by itself in 3 to 6 weeks. Stop the bleeding, rinse, cover with a non-stick dressing, and re-dress every day or two. Do not try to stitch skin over it.

### Bone showing
Keep it covered with a damp clean dressing so it does not dry out. Exposed bone needs a surgeon to trim and cover it. Until then, keep it clean.

### Pain and the weeks after
Pain reliever by the label. Many people feel the missing part itching or hurting. This is real and common, and usually fades. Gentle rubbing and wrapping of the stump helps. Start moving the joints above the stump within days so they do not stiffen.

### Tetanus
A dirty amputation needs a tetanus booster when care can be reached, if the last one was more than 5 years ago.

**Train:** Academy courses "Stop the Bleed" and "Casualty care in a crisis".

Source: University of Michigan Health and Kaiser Permanente (Healthwise), emergency care for an accidental amputation; Committee on Tactical Combat Casualty Care guidelines; Wilderness Medical Society.
