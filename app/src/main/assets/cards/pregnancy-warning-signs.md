1. Know the way to the nearest hospital before you need it.
2. **If phones work, call 911** or go to the hospital now for heavy vaginal bleeding, fluid leaking before 37 weeks, severe belly pain, a seizure, a severe headache that will not go away, vision changes, sudden swelling of the face or hands, chest pain or trouble breathing.
3. Call your clinician the same day for the baby moving much less than usual, fever, burning when you pass urine, constant vomiting, or regular contractions before 37 weeks.
4. Lie on your left side while waiting for help.

## If no help is coming

- **Heavy bleeding:** lie down, raise the feet, pad with clean cloths, and get to care by any means.
- **Severe headache, vision changes, swollen face:** may be pre-eclampsia, which can lead to seizures. Keep the person calm, on her left side, and move toward care.
- **Seizure:** protect from injury. See "Seizure".
- **Water broke early or contractions before 37 weeks:** rest, drink fluids, and move toward care. If birth begins, see "Baby coming and no help yet".
- **Baby movement:** lie on the left side and count movements for an hour. Fewer than 10 in two hours needs care.
- **Keep records** (due date, blood type, problems) in the go-bag.

## Know more

### Be ready early
In the last two months, know the route, keep fuel and a bag packed, and have a plan for who will help.

### Healthy habits
Drink water, eat regular meals, rest, and avoid smoke and alcohol.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".

Source: CDC (Hear Her campaign) and American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists guidance.
