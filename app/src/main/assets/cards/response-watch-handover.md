**The person going off watch stays responsible until the next person says "I have the watch." An unattended radio is not a watch. If phones work and anyone is in danger, call 911.**
1. **Is the next person here and fit for duty?** Rested, awake, and able to listen.
2. **Who is away from camp** right now, and when are they due back?
3. **Say the channels:** main and back-up. Do a radio check.
4. **The new person says "I have the watch."** Both sign or initial the log.

## If no help is coming

- **Check batteries and charging,** and pass on weather and hazards.
- **Pass on anything unfinished:** open problems, repairs, expected returns and check-ins.
- **Do not hand over by leaving.** Do not send the person on watch away unless someone else takes the listening post.
- **Short watches:** 2 to 4 hours keep people alert. A tired watch misses things.
- **If nobody is fit to take over,** wake someone. Never leave the post empty.

## Know more

### Why a handover
Most mistakes happen at the change of watch.

### Sleep
Rotate so everyone gets a long block of sleep.

**Train:** Academy course "Operator emergency management".
