**When thunder roars, go indoors.** If you can hear thunder, you can be hit.
1. Go into a **building with plumbing and wiring** or a hard-topped car with the windows up. Tents and open shelters do not protect you.
2. Stay inside **at least 30 minutes after the last thunder.**
3. Indoors, stay away from windows, doors, plumbing, corded phones and things plugged in.
4. **Someone struck by lightning carries no charge.** If phones work, call 911 and start CPR if they do not breathe normally. See "CPR: adult".

## If no help is coming

- **Outside with nothing safe near:** get off high ground and away from tall lone trees, water and metal. Crouch on the balls of your feet, feet together, hands over your ears. Do not lie flat.
- **Spread out** groups by 20 feet so one strike does not hit all.
- **Start CPR at once** on a person who is not breathing. The heart often restarts.
- **Treat burns and shock** and check for hearing and sight problems. See "Burns".
- **Flash to bang:** count seconds from flash to thunder. 30 seconds or less means go in now.

## Know more

### Myths
Rubber soles and metal-free clothing do not help. Open fields, trees and water are the worst places.

### Camping
Pick a low, dry spot with no tall lone trees. A hard-topped vehicle is best.
