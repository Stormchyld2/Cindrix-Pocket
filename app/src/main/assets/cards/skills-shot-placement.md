**Take only a shot you can make cleanly. Aim for the heart and lungs.**
1. Zero your rifle at 100 yards.
2. Find your range: the distance at which every shot stays in a pie-plate-sized circle.
3. Shoot from a rest or sitting, kneeling and standing.
4. Aim just behind the front leg, in the middle of a broadside deer.
5. Squeeze the trigger slowly and stay on target.
6. Follow up quietly: wait 30 minutes before tracking a hit animal, unless it is in sight.

## If you have less gear

- **No range:** practise dry-fire safely at home.

## Know more

### Do not
Take head, neck, running or brush shots.

### Stay inside
100 yards for most hunters.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", marksmanship lesson, and the Defensive marksmanship course.
