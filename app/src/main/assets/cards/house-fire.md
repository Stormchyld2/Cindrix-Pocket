**Fire can fill a home in about five minutes.**
1. **Get out. Stay out.** Do not stop for things. Do not go back in.
2. **Stay low** and crawl under the smoke.
3. **Feel the door** with the back of your hand. If it is hot, or smoke comes around it, keep it shut and use your second way out.
4. If you cannot get out: close the door, seal the gaps with cloth, and signal from a window.
5. **Clothes on fire:** stop, drop and roll.
6. **Call 911 from outside if phones work.** Meet at your family's meeting place and count everyone.

## If no help is coming

- **There may be no fire crew.** Your extinguisher, hose and bucket are the fire department. Use them only on a small fire, with your back to the exit.
- **Small fire:** P.A.S.S.: pull the pin, aim at the base, squeeze, sweep. A grease fire needs a lid, never water.
- **If it spreads, leave.** Property is not worth a life.
- **Burns:** cool for at least 10 minutes with clean water. See "Burns".
- **Smoke inhalation:** fresh air, sit up. Treat coughing or trouble breathing as serious.
- **After:** a fire watch for hours for flare-ups. Check the roof and attic.
- **Be ready:** two ways out of every room, alarms on each level, and practice twice a year.

## Know more

### Why smoke kills
Poison gas and heat, not flames, kill most people in house fires.

### Fire at the camp
Keep fires small and away from tents. Water and a shovel at hand. See "Starting a fire".

**Train:** Academy course "Home damage control".
