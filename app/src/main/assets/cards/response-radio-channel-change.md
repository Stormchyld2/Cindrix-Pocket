**Only change channels for a reason everyone agreed to. FRS channels are shared and not private. If phones work and anyone is in danger, call 911.**
1. **Decide it is needed:** the main channel is noisy, busy with strangers, or cannot be understood.
2. **The watch lead says it:** "All stations, moving to the back-up channel."
3. **Everyone who can hear answers** with their call sign, then switches.
4. **Base calls each person** to confirm contact.

## If no help is coming

- **Anyone who does not answer** is out of contact. See "Base radio failed or lost contact with a patrol".
- **Write the change in the log** and update the written channel card.
- **Privacy codes off** on the emergency channel. A privacy code is not encryption.
- **Never move alone** without an agreed reason.
- **If both channels fail,** use the paper plan: a fixed time and place to meet.

## Know more

### Why shared
Anyone with a radio can hear you. Say nothing you would not say aloud.

### Back-up plan
Write the channels on a card and carry it with the radio.

**Train:** Academy course "Communications".
