Pneumonia is a lung infection. It can follow a cold, flu or COVID-19, and it needs care.

**Signs:** cough, fever or chills, shortness of breath, chest pain when breathing or coughing, tiredness, and confusion (most of all in older people).

1. If you think it could be pneumonia, **get care promptly.** Do not wait it out.
2. Rest, sip fluids, and keep away from others.
3. Write down the temperature and when signs started.

**Call 911** for trouble breathing, chest pain or pressure that does not go away, new confusion, cannot wake up, or blue, gray or very pale lips or skin.

## If no help is coming

- **Count the breaths.** An adult at rest breathing more than 25 times a minute, or a child much faster than usual, is a serious sign.
- **Sit the person up,** loosen clothing, keep warm, and give small sips often.
- **Fever medicine** as the label says for age and weight. Never aspirin to a child.
- **Antibiotics** help only bacterial pneumonia and need a clinician. Do not use leftovers.
- **Move toward care** by any means if breathing is hard, lips are blue, or the person is confused.
- **Watch the high-risk:** older people, young children, smokers, and anyone with long-term illness.

## Know more

### The warning pattern
A cold or flu that gets better, then returns with fever and a worse cough.

### Preventing it
Hand washing, not smoking, clean air in the shelter, and staying warm and fed.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
