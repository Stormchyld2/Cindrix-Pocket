Filtering cloudy water makes it easier to treat, but **a pre-filter alone does not make it safe.** Always boil, disinfect or use a proper purifier afterwards.
1. **Let it settle** in a clean container for a few hours. Pour off the clearer water from the top.
2. **Strain through cloth.** A folded clean shirt, a bandana or coffee filters take out leaves, grit and sand.
3. **Layered filter** in a cut bottle or cloth bag, upside down: cloth at the neck, then charcoal from a clean wood fire (crushed, rinsed), then sand, then gravel. Pour through the top and catch at the neck.
4. **Run it through twice** if still cloudy.
5. **Boil it** at a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above about 6,500 feet), or use other methods. See "Making water safe: boil, filter, treat".
6. **Keep the filter clean.** Rinse the sand and gravel between uses and throw the charcoal away after one day.
7. **Store water** in a clean, covered container.

## If you have less gear

- **No bottle:** a cloth over a bucket or a hole in the ground beside a stream (a seep well) filters through sand.
- **No charcoal:** sand and cloth alone still help.
- **No pot to boil:** use bleach or sunlight. See "Make water safer with sunlight (SODIS)".

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Thinking clear means safe. Using dirty sand. Skipping the boil.

### Practice
Build one with a cut bottle and see how clear the water gets.
