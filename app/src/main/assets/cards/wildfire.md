1. **Leave early when told.** Do not wait to see flames. Take the go-bag, papers, medicines, pets and chargers. Fill the car with fuel.
2. If you have time: close windows and doors, turn off gas at the meter, move furniture away from windows, hook up garden hoses, and bring in outdoor things that burn.
3. Wear long sleeves, long pants, boots and a mask (an N95 helps with smoke).
4. **In the car:** windows closed and vents off. Park away from trees, engine running, lie on the floor and cover up.
5. **If phones work, call 911.** Say where you are.

## If no help is coming

- **Caught on foot:** go to a cleared area, a river or a bare patch. Lie low and face down, covering the head and face.
- **Stuck at home:** stay inside away from windows, doors closed, and fill sinks and tubs with water. Move to the side of the house away from the fire.
- **Fire fights:** the best defense is a cleared zone: rake leaves, cut dry grass, and move wood piles away.
- **Smoke:** stay inside with windows shut and rest. Wet cloth over nose and mouth helps a little. Move to cleaner air if breathing gets hard.
- **Burns and smoke inhalation:** see "Burns".
- **After:** go back only when safe. Watch for hot spots, ash and falling trees.

## Know more

### Why leave early
Fire can move faster than a person can run, and roads fill or burn.

### Home hardening
Metal roof, no gutters full of leaves, and screened vents.

**Train:** Academy course "Wildfire preparedness".

Source: Ready.gov and NIFC wildfire guidance.
