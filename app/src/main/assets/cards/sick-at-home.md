1. Keep the sick person in **one room,** with their own bathroom if possible. One person looks after them.
2. **Wash hands** with soap after any contact. Wear a mask around them and open a window when you can.
3. Keep them drinking fluids and resting. Separate cups and towels. Clean touched surfaces every day.
4. Keep a log: when it started, temperature, what they ate and drank.
5. **If phones work, call 911** for trouble breathing, chest pain, new confusion, blue lips or face, cannot wake, cannot keep fluids down, or a baby or older person getting worse.

## If no help is coming

- **Fluids first.** Small sips often. See "Dehydration and diarrhea".
- **Check the signs** every few hours: temperature, breathing rate, alertness, urine. Write them down.
- **Know the danger signs:** fast or hard breathing, blue lips, confusion, stiff neck with fever, no urine for 8 hours. Move toward care by any means.
- **Protect the carer:** one carer, gloves and mask for close care, and rest for them too.
- **Do not share medicine.** Follow the label for age and weight.
- **Isolate** as long as signs last. See "Stop a contagious illness from spreading".
- **Waste:** separate bucket, and bleach or hot soapy cleaning.

## Know more

### Common illnesses
Colds, flu, stomach bugs, and strep. See "A common cold", "The flu (influenza)" and "Vomiting".

### The log
It helps a clinician later and shows if the person is getting better or worse.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
