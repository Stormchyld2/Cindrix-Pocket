1. **Fear is normal.** Name it. Slow your breath: in for 4, out for 6.
2. **Do the next small job.** Water, shelter, warmth, signal. Lists keep the mind busy.
3. **Stay in groups.** Share tasks. Do not leave anyone alone, especially children, older people or anyone hurt.
4. **No big choices when tired, cold or hungry.** Rest, warm up and eat, then decide.
5. **Keep morale up:** talk, sing, tell stories, share jobs, and recognize each person's effort.
6. **Plan for the night** before it gets dark.
7. **Sleep is part of the plan.** Take turns on watch.
8. **If someone is in a panic,** stay close, speak low and calm, and give them a simple job.

If you or someone you know is thinking of suicide or in crisis, call or text **988** (US).

## If you have less gear

- **No light:** keep a fire, and sit with your back to a tree or rock.
- **No shelter:** group together and share warmth. See "Staying warm: C.O.L.D.".

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Going out alone in the dark, making choices in a panic, and staying silent.

### Practice
Spend a night out in a safe place to learn the sounds and what fear feels like.

**Train:** Academy course "Survival mindset".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA), 988.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
