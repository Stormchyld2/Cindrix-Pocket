1. **Outdoors only,** more than **20 feet** from windows, doors and vents, exhaust pointing away. Never in a garage, basement or porch, even with the door open.
2. Keep it dry under a canopy. Never in rain or standing water.
3. Let it **cool before refueling.** Store fuel outside living areas in approved containers.
4. Plug appliances in directly or use heavy-duty outdoor cords. **Never plug a generator into a wall outlet.**
5. Keep a battery CO alarm near the bedrooms. If anyone feels dizzy or sick, get outside and **if phones work, call 911.**

## If no help is coming

- **Fuel is the limit.** Store only what you can rotate safely, and run the generator only a few hours a day for freezer, charging and pumps.
- **Let it rest:** stop to cool, check oil, and refuel only when cool.
- **Spare parts:** oil, filter, spark plug, and a manual.
- **Transfer switch:** a licensed electrician can fit one. Without it, use cords only.
- **Poisoning signs:** headache, dizziness, nausea. See "Carbon monoxide".
- **Quiet and light:** a running generator draws attention. Place it out of sight and use ear protection.
- **Fire:** keep a fire extinguisher near.

## Know more

### Why 20 feet
Exhaust sneaks in through gaps, doors and vents and can build up to deadly levels.

### Stored fuel
Gasoline spoils in months. Add a stabilizer. Rotate.

**Train:** Academy course "Electricity and batteries".
