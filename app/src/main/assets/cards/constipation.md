1. **Drink more water.** Add fruit, vegetables, beans and whole grains if you have them.
2. Move around: a walk helps the gut work.
3. Do not strain hard or hold it in. Sit at the same time each day, after a meal.
4. Use a laxative only as the label says, and only for a short time.

**See a doctor if one can be reached** for blood in the stool, severe belly pain, vomiting, a swollen hard belly, no gas or stool for days, or constipation after a fall or injury. In an emergency, call 911 if phones work.

## If no help is coming

- **Expect it.** A change to stored food, less water, stress, less privacy and pain medicine all slow the gut. It is one of the most common complaints in any camp.
- **Water first:** several extra cups a day. A warm drink on waking often starts things moving.
- **Add bulk:** oats, beans, dried fruit, bran, whole grain, vegetables. Prunes or prune juice work well.
- **Squat.** Knees above the hips, leaning forward, makes passing stool far easier than sitting upright.
- **Hard stool stuck low down:** a spoonful of cooking oil or mineral oil by mouth at night, or a little petroleum jelly worked just inside the opening, softens the way.
- **Getting worse:** the belly swelling tight, vomiting, cramping in waves, and passing no wind at all. That is a blockage, not simple constipation. Nothing by mouth but sips, and get to a surgeon if one can be reached. See the card "Severe belly pain".

## Know more

### Pain medicine
Strong pain medicine (opioids) nearly always causes constipation. Start extra water and fruit on the first day of taking it.

### Children and old people
A child who holds it in because it hurts needs more water, fruit, and a regular unhurried sitting time. Old people who move little need the same, plus help to walk each day. Liquid stool leaking around a hard plug can look like diarrhea.

### Piles and small tears
Bright red blood on the paper with pain on passing stool is usually a small tear or piles from straining. Soften the stool, wash with warm water after each visit, and it heals. Dark or mixed-in blood is a different matter and needs a doctor.

### Have ready
Oats, beans and dried fruit in the food store, a bulk fiber supplement, and a mild laxative.
