**Boots decide how far you can walk. Mend them early.**
1. Clean the old stitching.
2. Pierce the holes with an awl.
3. Sew with a saddle stitch: two needles and one waxed thread.
4. For a loose sole, rough both surfaces, add contact cement, press hard and clamp for a day.
5. Dry leather slowly, never by a fire, and treat it with wax or oil.

## If you have less gear

- **No awl:** use a thick nail.

## Know more

### Keep
Old belts, boots and straps. Leather is hard to replace.

### Practice
The drill "Mend a boot, belt or strap".

**Train:** The Academy course "Trades and repair".
