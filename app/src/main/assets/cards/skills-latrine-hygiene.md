1. **Dig a cathole** at least 6 to 8 inches (15 to 20 cm) deep and **at least 200 feet (60 m)** from water, trails and camp. Cover it afterward.
2. **Wash your hands** after the toilet and before eating or handling food, with soap and **safe water.** Without water, use **hand sanitizer with at least 60 percent alcohol.**
3. **For a group staying longer,** dig a trench latrine downhill and away from camp and water, and cover each use with soil.
4. **Keep water, food and waste separate.** Keep animals out of food.
5. **Wash dishes** with hot, clean water and soap. Let them air dry.
6. **Keep feet dry and clean.** Change socks and treat blisters early.
7. **Burn or pack out** trash. Do not bury food waste where animals can dig.
8. **Sick people** use their own bucket or area, and wash hands carefully.

## If you have less gear

- **No soap:** use clean ash or sand and water. Scrub hard for 20 seconds.
- **No shovel:** a flat stone, a stick, or a tin lid will dig a small hole.
- **No water:** a leaf or cloth, and then burn or bury it.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
A latrine too close to the water, no hand washing station, and no cover.

### Practice
Set up a hand washing station at camp and use it for a week.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC and Leave No Trace guidance.
