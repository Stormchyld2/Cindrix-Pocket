1. **Rule of threes:** about 3 days without water, so look early.
2. **Water runs downhill.** Follow valleys, gullies and animal trails downhill. Green plants often mean water.
3. **Streams, rivers and lakes** are best. Take water from moving water, from the middle of a stream.
4. **Collect rain** with a tarp, poncho or bowl. Collect dew by wiping grass with a cloth and wringing it out.
5. **Snow and ice:** melt first. Eating snow lowers body heat.
6. **Dry riverbeds:** dig at the outside bend of a curve.
7. **Do not drink urine, sea water or alcohol.**
8. **Treat all outdoor water.** Clear water can still make you ill. See "Making water safe: boil, filter, treat".

## If you have less gear

- **No container:** a cloth, a hat, a plastic bag or a cupped leaf.
- **No pot to melt snow:** a tin can, a bottle held near the fire (not on it).
- **Solar still or plant bag:** slow, little water. Use only when no better source.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Waiting too long, drinking still pools, and trusting clear water.

### Practice
Find three water sources on your local map and visit them.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".
