**Nuts are the best wild calories. Learn them now and store them.**
1. Gather hickory and walnut in fall after they drop.
2. Let walnut husks rot or dry, wearing gloves, because they stain.
3. Crack the shells with a hammer on a stone.
4. Pick out the kernels.
5. Store dry in the shell, in a cool, dry, rodent-proof place.

## If you have less gear

- **No hammer:** use a rock on a flat stone.
- **No time:** eat what you can and dry the rest.

## Know more

### Look-alikes
**Buckeye** (horse chestnut) is poisonous. It has palm-like leaves of five leaflets and a smooth brown nut with a pale spot; hickory has pinnate leaves and a husk that splits in four.

### When
September to November.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".
