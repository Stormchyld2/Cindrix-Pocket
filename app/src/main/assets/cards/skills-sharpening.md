**A sharp tool is safer than a dull one.**
1. Wet a whetstone with water or oil.
2. Hold the knife at about 20 degrees to the stone.
3. Push the edge across as if slicing a thin layer, with the same angle and number of strokes on each side.
4. Finish on a strop and test on paper.
5. File an axe from the blade toward the edge, away from your body.

## If you have less gear

- **No stone:** use the unglazed rim of a ceramic mug, or a flat rock.

## Know more

### Safety
Keep fingers behind the edge.

### Practice
The drill "Sharpen a knife and an axe".
