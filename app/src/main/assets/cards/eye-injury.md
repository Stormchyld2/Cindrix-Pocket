**Chemical in the eye:**
1. **Rinse right away** with clean lukewarm water for 15 to 20 minutes. Hold the eye open. Tilt the head so the water runs away from the other eye.
2. Take out contact lenses if they come out easily.
3. If phones work, call Poison Help (1-800-222-1222).

**Something stuck in the eye, a cut, or a hard blow:**
1. Do not rub. Do not try to pull out anything stuck in the eyeball.
2. Cover the eye with a paper cup or shield taped in place. Put no pressure on it.
3. Cover both eyes to stop them moving, and get care if it can be reached.

## If no help is coming

- **Chemicals:** keep rinsing. For lye, cement, lime or drain cleaner, rinse for a full hour. These keep burning long after the pain eases.
- **Loose grit or an eyelash:** pull the upper lid out and down over the lower one, blink under clean water, or lift the speck off the white of the eye or the inside of the lid with the corner of a clean wet cloth.
- **Something stuck in the eyeball itself, or a cut eye:** do not remove it. Shield the eye with a cup, cover the other eye too, keep the person lying back with the head raised, and give nothing that makes them vomit or strain. This needs an eye surgeon.
- **A scratch** feels like sand under the lid, with tears and pain in light. It heals in 1 to 3 days. Rest the eye, keep hands away, and wear dark glasses. Do not patch it shut for days.
- **Check sight twice a day:** can they count fingers, read print, see the same on both sides.
- **Getting worse:** sight dimming, deep aching pain, pus, the colored part going cloudy, a pool of blood in front of the colored part, pain with fever.

## Know more

### A blow to the eye
A black eye alone heals. Cold cloths for the first day. Danger signs: double vision, cannot look up, a sunken or bulging eye, blood inside the eye, or loss of sight. Rest sitting up, no straining, no pain relievers that thin the blood.

### Snow blindness and welding flash
Gritty, burning, watering eyes 6 to 12 hours after bright snow, water or arc light. It is a sunburn of the eye and heals in 2 days. Rest in a dark place with cold cloths. Prevent it with sunglasses, or goggles made by cutting thin slits in bark or card.

### Infection
A red eye with sticky yellow discharge, lids stuck in the morning. See the card "Pink eye (conjunctivitis)".

### Contact lenses
Take them out at the first redness or pain and wear glasses. A red, painful eye in a lens wearer can be a serious infection.

### Have ready
Safety glasses for chopping, grinding and chemicals, sunglasses for everyone, a clean squeeze bottle for rinsing, and an eye shield.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
