**Wear gloves, cook it to 165°F, and avoid any animal that looks sick.**
1. Hunt in season with a licence.
2. Wear gloves to skin and clean the animal.
3. Cut across the back, pull both ways, and take out the guts.
4. Wash the meat in clean water.
5. Cook to **165°F**, until well done.

## If you have less gear

- **No knife:** use a sharp rock edge.

## Know more

### Warning
Rabbits can carry tularemia. Avoid animals that moved slowly or have spots on the liver.

### When
Squirrel season starts in late summer; rabbit in fall.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", game and fish module.

Source: WV Division of Natural Resources and Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency; USDA FSIS safe temperatures

Reference only, not medical advice. Where help can be reached, call 911.
