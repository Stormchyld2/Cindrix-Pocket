1. **Switch to the emergency or distress channel.** Marine VHF: **channel 16.** GMRS or FRS: the channel your group agreed on. Listen first.
2. **Press and speak slowly and clearly.** Say **"MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY"** only if life is in danger. Otherwise **"PAN-PAN"** three times.
3. Give: **who you are, where you are** (landmark, map position, coordinates), **what is wrong, how many people** and **what you need.**
4. **Release the button and listen.** Repeat every few minutes.
5. **Save the battery:** off between calls, or low power.
6. **Antenna upright,** and go to a high open place.
7. **A hand-crank or battery weather radio** gives warnings (NOAA Weather Radio).

## If you have less gear

- **No radio:** use a whistle, mirror and fire. See "Signalling for rescue".
- **A phone that cannot call** may still send a text.
- **A repeater or higher ground** carries a weak radio farther.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Speaking too fast, no location, calling MAYDAY for a small problem, and not listening for the reply.

### Practice
Run a practice call on your group channel each month.

**Train:** Academy course "Communications".
