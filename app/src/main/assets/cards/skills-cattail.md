**A cattail from clean water gives food in every season.**
1. Find **cattails** in clean marshes and pond edges: tall, with brown cigar-like heads.
2. In spring, pull up young shoots and eat the white cores raw or cooked.
3. In early summer, boil the green flower spikes like corn.
4. In fall and winter, dig the starchy roots, wash and dry them, and grind for flour.
5. Take from clean water only.

## If you have less gear

- **No pot:** young cores can be eaten raw.

## Know more

### Look-alikes
**Young wild iris** looks like young cattail but is poisonous. A cattail leaf is flat and has a smell like cucumber; iris leaves are flat and fan-shaped. Do not pick without being sure.

### Warning
Plants absorb pollution. Avoid roadside ditches and runoff.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".
