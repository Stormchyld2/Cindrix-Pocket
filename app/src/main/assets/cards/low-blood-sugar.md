Signs: shaky, sweaty, hungry, confused, pale, fast heartbeat, headache, cranky, acting drunk.

1. **If the person is awake and can swallow safely**, give about 15 grams of fast sugar: 3 to 4 glucose tablets, or about 4 oz (½ cup) of juice or regular soda, or a tablespoon of honey or sugar.
2. **Wait 15 minutes and check again.** If blood sugar is still below 70 mg/dL, or they still have signs, give the same again.
3. **Once it is above 70 mg/dL**, give a snack or meal with starch and protein.
4. **If the person is confused, cannot swallow or is unconscious:** put no food or drink in their mouth. Use glucagon if you have it, **as directed on the package**, turn them on their side, and call 911 if phones work.

Insulin and other medicine amounts are not given here. Follow your doctor's written plan.

## If no help is coming

- **When unsure whether sugar is low or high, treat it as low.** Sugar given to a person with high sugar does little harm. Sugar withheld from a person with low sugar can kill.
- **Cannot swallow and no glucagon:** lay them on their side and rub a little honey, syrup or sugar paste onto the inside of the cheek and gums, a small smear at a time, so it cannot run down the throat. Repeat every few minutes.
- **After they wake,** they must eat a real meal, and they stay with someone for the next few hours. Low sugar often comes back, especially after long-acting insulin, exercise or alcohol.
- **Find out why:** a missed meal, more work than usual, too much insulin for the food eaten, illness, or alcohol. Change the plan so it does not repeat.
- **Not waking within 15 minutes of treatment** is very serious. Keep them on their side, keep giving sugar inside the cheek, and get to help if any can be reached.

## Know more

### No meter
Go by the signs. Shaking, sweating and sudden bad temper or confusion in a person on insulin or diabetes tablets is low sugar until proven otherwise.

### At night
Restless sleep, sweating, nightmares or a headache on waking. Give a bedtime snack with starch and protein after a day of hard work.

### People who do not feel it coming
Some long-term diabetics lose the early warning signs. Others in the group need to know what that person looks like when going low, and act for them.

### Not only diabetics
Heavy drinking without food, long hard effort without eating, and severe illness in small children can all cause low sugar.

### Have ready
Glucose tablets or hard candy in every pocket and pack of a person on insulin, honey in the kit, a glucagon kit if prescribed, and a note in the wallet that says the person is diabetic.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
