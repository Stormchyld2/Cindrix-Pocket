Signs of high sugar: very thirsty, passing a lot of urine, tired, blurry sight, dry mouth.

1. Check blood sugar. Drink water or other sugar-free fluids.
2. If you have ketone strips and sugar is high or you are sick, check for ketones.
3. **Follow your doctor's written plan** for what to do about medicine. Do not exercise hard if ketones are high.
4. **It is an emergency** (call 911 if phones work) for signs of diabetic ketoacidosis: nausea and vomiting, belly pain, fruity-smelling breath, fast deep breathing, drowsy or confused.

Insulin and other medicine amounts are not given here.

## If no help is coming

- **Water is the first treatment.** High sugar pulls water out of the body through the urine. Drink water or unsweetened rehydration drink steadily, a cup every hour while awake.
- **Keep taking insulin** as the doctor's sick-day plan says, even when not eating. Stopping insulin during illness is the most common cause of ketoacidosis.
- **Look for what set it off:** an infection (chest, urine, skin, tooth, foot), missed medicine, weakened insulin, or stress and injury. Treat the cause.
- **Check every 2 to 4 hours:** sugar and ketones if you have strips, breathing, alertness, urine.
- **Getting worse:** vomiting that will not stop, deep sighing breaths, fruity breath, belly pain, sleepiness. This is ketoacidosis. Without insulin and fluids it is fatal, so it is a reason to travel to a hospital at once.
- **Getting better:** thirst easing, less urine, clearer head, ketones clearing.

## Know more

### No meter or strips
Extreme thirst, passing urine constantly, weight dropping, blurry sight and tiredness mean sugar is high. Urine that draws ants, or that smells sweet or of nail polish remover, is another sign.

### Type 1 and type 2 behave differently
Type 1 (the body makes no insulin) can slide into ketoacidosis within a day. Type 2 more often creeps up over days into severe dehydration and confusion without ketones, mostly in older people. Both need water and their medicine.

### Bringing it down without more medicine
Less starch and sugar at each meal, more water, and gentle walking after meals if ketones are not high. These help type 2 a good deal and type 1 only a little.

### Weakened insulin
If readings rise with no other cause, the insulin may have been spoiled by heat. Open a fresh vial. See the card "Diabetes when the power is out".
