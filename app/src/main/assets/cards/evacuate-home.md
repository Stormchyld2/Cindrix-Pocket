1. **Listen to officials** and go when told. Leaving early beats leaving late.
2. Grab the **go-bag**, medicines, glasses, phones and chargers, papers, cash, and pets with carriers.
3. Wear sturdy shoes and clothing for the weather.
4. Turn off gas, water and power **only if officials tell you to**. Lock up. Leave a note saying where you went.
5. **Use the routes officials give.** Keep the tank at least half full.
6. **If phones work,** text your out-of-town contact where you are going. Call 911 only for emergencies.

## If no help is coming

- **Go early,** before roads jam. One night's delay can mean hours in traffic.
- **Have two routes** and a rally point in each direction. See "Choose rally points before you need them".
- **Fuel, water, food:** carry what you need for several days. Do not count on stops.
- **Leave a note** inside the door, and tell a neighbor.
- **Pets** go with you or have food and water left out. Never tied.
- **Cannot drive:** a loaded cart or packs and a walk. See "Moving as a group on foot".
- **Do not return** until it is safe. If you cannot get back, you need a second shelter plan.

## Know more

### Go-bag check
Water, food, medicines, light, radio, papers, cash, a map, and clothes. See "What to carry: ten essentials".

### Practice
Drill once a year. Time how long it takes.

**Train:** Academy course "First 72 hours".

Source: Ready.gov evacuation guidance.

Reference only, not medical advice. Where help can be reached, call 911.
