CO has no smell and no color. Signs: headache, dizzy, weak, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, confusion. People asleep can die before they notice.
1. If an alarm sounds or you think you have it: **get everyone, including pets, outside into fresh air now.**
2. **If phones work, call 911.** Do not go back in.
3. Anyone with signs needs medical care, even if they feel better outside.

## If no help is coming

- **Fresh air is the treatment.** Keep the person outside, sitting up, warm, and watched. Rest.
- **Do not go back in** until the source is off and the room is aired for a long time.
- **Find the cause:** a generator, grill, camp stove, charcoal, car in a garage, or blocked chimney or furnace.
- **Battery alarms** near every sleeping area. Test them. Spare batteries.
- **Heating:** never heat with a gas oven, grill, or camp stove indoors. A stove designed for indoors, with a working chimney, is the way.
- **Signs in a group:** several people with headaches at once means CO until proven otherwise.
- **Unconscious:** get them out, check breathing, start CPR if not. See "CPR: adult".

## Know more

### Why it kills
It takes the place of oxygen in the blood.

### Generators
Outdoors, more than 20 feet away, exhaust pointing away. See "Using a generator safely".

**Train:** Academy course "Power outage preparedness".
