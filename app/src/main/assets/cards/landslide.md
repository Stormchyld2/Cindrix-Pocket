**Warning signs:** new cracks in the ground or walls, doors that stick, tilting trees or poles, a new spring or muddy water, a rumble that grows after heavy rain.

1. **Leave at once** and go to higher ground away from the slope, not along the valley.
2. Tell neighbors. **If phones work, call 911.**
3. **If you cannot get away,** curl into a tight ball and protect your head.

## If no help is coming

- **Leave when rain is heavy and long.** Slides often come in the night. Do not wait for warnings.
- **After:** stay away from the slide. More may follow. Check for people without entering the slide area.
- **Look for hazards:** broken power, water and gas lines. See "Gas leak".
- **Injured:** treat for bleeding and spine injury. See "Neck or back injury".
- **Do not return** for days. Ask someone who knows the slope.
- **Plan the route:** two ways out that do not cross the slope or a stream.

## Know more

### Where they happen
Steep slopes after fires, heavy rain, or a thaw. Burn scars are very risky.

### Rivers
A slide can dam a stream and burst. Move away from valley floors.



**Train:** Academy course "Home damage control".
