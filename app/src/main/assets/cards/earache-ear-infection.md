**Signs:** ear pain, fever, a fussy child, rubbing or tugging at the ear, trouble sleeping. It often follows a cold.

1. Rest and give extra fluids.
2. Pain and fever medicine eases the pain but does not cure the infection. **Follow the label.** Babies under 6 months can have only acetaminophen.
3. **Never give aspirin to a child or teen.**
4. A warm cloth held on the ear helps.

**See a doctor if one can be reached** for a fever of 102.2°F (39°C) or higher, pus or fluid from the ear, pain that gets worse, signs lasting more than 2 to 3 days, or any hearing loss. **A baby under 3 months with a fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher needs care right away.** In an emergency, call 911 if phones work.

**Do not** put drops, oil or cotton into an ear that is draining fluid, or poke anything into the ear.

## If no help is coming

- **Most ear infections clear by themselves in 2 to 3 days.** Pain relief, warmth and rest are usually all that is needed.
- **Sleep with the sore ear up** and the head raised on a pillow.
- **If the eardrum bursts,** pus or bloody fluid runs out and the pain suddenly eases. This is common and usually heals in a few weeks. Wipe the outside only and keep water out of the ear.
- **If the group holds antibiotics supplied by a doctor for this,** the time to use them as directed is severe pain with high fever, both ears in a child under 2, a draining ear, or no improvement after 3 days.
- **Getting worse:** swelling, redness and tenderness of the bone behind the ear with the ear pushed forward; a stiff neck; severe headache; dizziness with vomiting; a drooping face. These mean the infection has spread and need a hospital.

## Know more

### Outer ear (swimmer's ear)
Pain when the ear is pulled or pressed, itching, and a swollen canal, after swimming or poking with something. Keep it dry. A few drops of half white vinegar, half clean water, twice a day, helps if the eardrum is whole (no drainage, no sudden hearing loss).

### Not an infection
Earache with a sore jaw, a bad tooth or a sore throat can be pain felt in the ear. Pressure pain after a cold or a change in height eases with yawning and swallowing.

### Wax
Dull hearing and fullness without fever. A few drops of warm cooking oil nightly for some days softens it. Never dig.

### Children
Feeding a baby lying flat and smoke in the air both raise the risk. Feed upright and keep children out of the smoke.
