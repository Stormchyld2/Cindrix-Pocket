**At home:**
1. Stay inside. Dress in layers. Close off rooms you do not need.
2. Never heat with an oven, grill or camp stove. Use alarms for carbon monoxide.
3. If the power fails, see "Power outage".

**Stuck in the car:**
1. **Stay in the car.** It shelters you and is easier for rescuers to find.
2. Tie a bright cloth to the antenna. **If phones work, call 911.**
3. **Run the engine about 10 minutes each hour** for heat, with the **tailpipe clear of snow** and one window cracked.
4. Move arms and legs. Huddle together.

## If no help is coming

- **Stay put** unless help is clearly close. Walking in a whiteout is how people die.
- **Stay warm:** layers, dry socks, hat, cover from the ground, and share heat. See "Staying warm: C.O.L.D.".
- **Signal:** a bright cloth, a mirror, the horn in short bursts, and a fire if you can.
- **Food and water:** eat and drink. Snow must be melted first. Do not eat it.
- **In the house:** pick one room, close the rest, hang blankets over windows, and burn only safe fuels. See "Carbon monoxide".
- **Pipes:** drip taps and open cabinet doors.
- **Cold injuries:** see "Hypothermia and frostbite".
- **Shoveling:** go slowly. Stop if your chest hurts. See "Heart attack".

## Know more

### Car kit
Blankets, candles in a can, food, water, a shovel, a light and a bag of sand.

### Fuel
Keep the tank above half in winter.

**Train:** Academy course "Cold-weather living".
