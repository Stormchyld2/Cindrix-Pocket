1. **Rest in shade** in the hottest part of the day and travel early morning and evening.
2. **Drink small amounts often.** Do not wait until thirsty. Pale urine is a good sign.
3. **Wear light, loose, light-colored clothes** and a wide hat. Cover skin.
4. **Do not ration water by drinking less.** Save effort by resting in shade.
5. **Eat salty food** with water when sweating heavily.
6. **Watch for signs:** headache, dizziness, nausea, cramps, flushed skin. Rest, shade and water. See "Heat exhaustion and heat stroke".
7. **Never leave anyone in a closed car.**

## If you have less gear

- **No shade:** a tarp, a blanket on poles, or a hole dug a foot or two deep gives relief. Raise the cover a foot or more above you to let air through.
- **No water to spare:** rest, do not sweat. Find water early. See "Finding water outdoors".
- **Wet cloth** on neck and head cools fast.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Hiking at noon, dark clothes, drinking too little, and no salt when sweating hard.

### Practice
Plan a day's walk with rest in the heat of the day.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".

Source: US Army Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76), paraphrased; CDC extreme heat guidance.

Reference only. Practise these skills before you need them.
