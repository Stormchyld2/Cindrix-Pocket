**Best: boil.**
1. Strain cloudy water through a clean cloth or coffee filter.
2. Bring it to a **rolling boil for 1 minute** (3 minutes above 6,500 feet / 2,000 m). Let it cool.
3. Store in clean, covered containers and write the date on them.

**No fuel: household bleach.** Use plain, unscented bleach with 5% to 9% sodium hypochlorite. For **clear water: 8 drops per gallon** (2 drops per quart or liter). Stir and **wait 30 minutes.** For cloudy or very cold water use **double**. It should smell slightly of chlorine.

If sick from water and phones work, call 911 for severe illness.

## If no help is coming

- **Clear it first.** Settle cloudy water, strain it through cloth, sand or charcoal, then treat. See "Clear cloudy water before you treat it".
- **Filters** remove germs but not viruses unless they say so. Add bleach or boil after.
- **Tablets:** follow the label. Chlorine and iodine do not kill cryptosporidium. Avoid iodine if pregnant or with thyroid problems.
- **Sun:** clear bottles in strong sun for 6 hours (2 days if cloudy). See "Make water safer with sunlight (SODIS)".
- **Use safe water** for drinking, brushing teeth, dishes, washing food and ice.
- **Finding it:** see "Finding water outdoors" and "Plan your water on the move".
- **Sick from water:** see "Dehydration and diarrhea".

## Know more

### Why boil
Heat kills all germs: bacteria, viruses and parasites. It does not remove chemicals.

### Bleach
Check the label for strength and date. Old bleach loses strength.

### Storing
Clean, covered, dark containers. Rotate every 6 months.

**Train:** Academy course "Water and sanitation".
