**Be where the animal is, downwind and unseen. Know your target and what is beyond it.**
1. Pass **hunter education** and buy your licence and tags.
2. Tell someone where you will be and when you will be back.
3. Wear **blaze orange** in gun seasons.
4. Hunt with the wind in your face or crosswise.
5. Sit still near a trail, water or food, before first light. Or move a few steps, then stop and watch for a long time.
6. Identify your target and the backstop before you shoot.

## If you have less gear

- **No stand:** sit against a tree with your back covered.
- **No rifle:** snares and traps may be legal, so check the rules.

## Know more

### Four rules
Treat every gun as loaded. Never point it at anything you do not mean to shoot. Keep your finger off the trigger until ready. Know your target and beyond.

### Seasons and licences
West Virginia (WVDNR) and Tennessee (TWRA) set seasons, limits and tags. Check yearly.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", game and fish module.
