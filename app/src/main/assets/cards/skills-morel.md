**Slice every morel from tip to base: a true morel is hollow all the way through.**
1. Look in spring (April to May) near dying elms, tulip poplars and old apple trees.
2. The cap is a honeycomb of pits fixed to the stem.
3. **Slice it lengthwise.** It must be completely hollow, from tip to base.
4. Cook all morels well. Never eat them raw.
5. Eat a small amount first.

## If you have less gear

- **No knife:** tear it in half.

## Know more

### False morel (Gyromitra)
A cap that is wrinkled and brain-like, or **cottony or chambered inside**, is a false morel and is poisonous. Do not eat it.

### Warning
Mushroom sickness 6 hours or more after eating is the most dangerous kind. Call poison control at 1-800-222-1222.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians", mushroom lesson.
