1. **Stop heavy bleeding first.** See "Severe bleeding".
2. **Clean the wound** once bleeding is controlled: rinse with plenty of clean, safe water. Remove dirt gently.
3. **Cover** with a clean dressing. Change it daily, or when wet or dirty.
4. **Watch for infection:** spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, fever or a red streak up the limb. Get help quickly. See "Signs a wound is infected".
5. **Keep the injured part clean and rest it.**
6. **Prevent blisters:** change socks, fit boots well, and tape a hot spot at the first sign.
7. **Deep wounds, animal bites and punctures** need a doctor, even if they seem small.
8. **Tetanus:** keep your shots up to date.

## If you have less gear

- **No dressing:** a clean cloth boiled or ironed, or a clean sock.
- **No clean water:** boil and cool it, or use treated water.
- **No tape:** strips of cloth tied around.

## Know more

### Common mistakes
Closing a dirty wound, leaving a dressing on too long, and ignoring early infection.

### Practice
Practice dressing a wound on a friend with what you have.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
