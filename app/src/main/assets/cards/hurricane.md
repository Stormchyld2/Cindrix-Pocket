**Before:** know your evacuation zone. Keep several days of food, water and medicines, papers in a waterproof box, and a plan that uses text messages. Tie down outdoor things and cover windows.

**If told to evacuate, go.** Take the go-bag, papers, medicines and pets. Never drive around barricades.

**If you stay:**
1. Shelter in a small interior room on the lowest floor that will not flood, away from windows.
2. If water rises, go to the highest floor. Do not enter a closed attic.
3. Do not go out in the eye. The storm will come back.
4. **If phones work, call 911** for life-threatening problems only. Texts use less battery.

## If no help is coming

- **Leave early:** a storm surge is the main killer. Do not wait for the last order.
- **Store water:** a gallon per person per day, plus a way to purify. Fill tubs before the storm.
- **After:** no help may reach you for days. Stay out of floodwater, treat all water as unsafe, and watch for downed lines.
- **Run a generator, grill or camp stove only outdoors,** at least 20 feet from windows, doors and the garage. See "Using a generator safely" and "Carbon monoxide".
- **Food:** eat the fridge food first, then the freezer, then dry goods. Throw out anything warm for more than 4 hours.
- **Cleanup:** boots, gloves, mask. Document damage with photos.

## Know more

### Hurricane categories
Wind is not the main danger. Water is.

### Pets
Take them or leave water and food. Never tie them up.

**Train:** Academy course "Home damage control".
