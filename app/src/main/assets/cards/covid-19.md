**Signs:** fever or chills, cough, shortness of breath, sore throat, stuffy or runny nose, new loss of taste or smell, tiredness, body aches, headache, nausea or vomiting, diarrhea. They show up 2 to 14 days after contact. Signs alone cannot tell it from the flu, so test if you can.

1. **Stay away from others**, including people in the house who are well, while you have signs.
2. Rest and drink plenty of fluids. Wear a well-fitted mask around others and keep fresh air moving.
3. **Higher risk** (older, pregnant, a long-term illness, a weak immune system): contact a health care provider right away if one can be reached. Treatment works best started within 5 to 7 days.
4. **Going back to normal:** when, for at least 24 hours, you are getting better overall AND have had no fever without fever medicine. Take extra care around others for 5 more days.

**It is an emergency** (call 911 if phones work) for trouble breathing, chest pain or pressure that does not go away, new confusion, cannot wake or stay awake, or pale, gray or blue lips.

## If no help is coming

- **Most people recover with rest, fluids and time,** in 1 to 2 weeks.
- **The danger period is days 5 to 10,** when some people who seemed to be improving get short of breath. Check breathing twice a day through that period.
- **Count breaths per minute at rest.** Over 24 in an adult, or being unable to finish a sentence, is a warning.
- **Lying face down** for an hour or two at a time, then on each side, helps the lungs take in more air. Change position through the day.
- **Keep them drinking and eating,** sitting up for meals, and moving the legs often. Lying still for days invites clots and chest infection.
- **Getting worse:** breathing faster each day, blue lips, confusion, chest pain. This needs oxygen, which only a hospital has.
- **Keep it from spreading through the group.** See the cards "Stop a contagious illness from spreading" and "Someone is sick at home".

## Know more

### Fever and aches
A fever reducer by the label, light clothing, and cool cloths. See the card "Fever in an adult".

### Cough and sore throat
Honey, warm drinks, salt-water gargle, steam.

### After it
Tiredness and breathlessness on effort can last for weeks. Return to heavy work in stages. Chest pain or a pounding heart on effort means stop and rest longer.

### Who to protect
The old, the pregnant, and anyone with heart, lung or kidney disease or diabetes. They should be kept apart from the sick person and looked after by someone else.

**Train:** Academy course "Family medical care without a clinic".
