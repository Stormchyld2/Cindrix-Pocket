**Eat these only when soft and fully ripe.**
1. Find **pawpaw** in rich bottomlands: a small tree with large drooping leaves and green-yellow fruit in late summer and fall.
2. Find **persimmon**: a tree with deeply checkered bark and orange fruit that ripens after frost.
3. Eat the pulp only. Spit out the seeds and skins.
4. Try a small amount first.

## If you have less gear

- **No tree ID:** go with a person who knows them.
- **Unripe:** leave it a few more days.

## Know more

### Warnings
Unripe persimmon is harshly bitter and can cause stomach blockages. A few people react to pawpaw.

### When
September to November.

**Train:** The Academy course "Wild food of the Appalachians".

Source: WVU Extension and University of Tennessee Extension foraging guides; Peterson Field Guide to Edible Wild Plants
