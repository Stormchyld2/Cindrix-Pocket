1. Sit and rest quietly for 5 minutes. Check again with the cuff on bare skin, arm at heart level.
2. Take your prescribed medicines as directed. Do not take extra.
3. Avoid salt, caffeine and smoking. Write down the readings and the time.

**If phones work, call 911** for a reading of 180/120 or higher with chest pain, trouble breathing, back pain, numbness, weakness, trouble speaking or vision changes. Without those signs, call a doctor right away.

## If no help is coming

- **A high number with no signs** is usually not a crisis. Rest, recheck after 10 to 15 minutes, and take only your usual medicine.
- **A high number with signs** (chest pain, weakness on one side, speech or vision change, confusion, severe headache) is an emergency. Treat as a stroke or heart attack. See "Stroke: BE FAST" and "Heart attack". Keep the person calm, sitting up, and get to care by any means.
- **Missed medicine is the most common cause.** Take the next regular dose, never a double.
- **Keep a log:** readings, time, and what was taken, to show a clinician later.
- **Plan ahead:** a cuff, a month or more of the usual medicine, and a low-salt food store.

## Know more

### Measuring well
Sit for 5 minutes, feet flat, back supported, arm resting at heart level. No caffeine or smoking for 30 minutes before.

### What to avoid
Salty and processed food, heavy alcohol, decongestants, and stopping blood pressure medicine suddenly.

### Long grid-down risk
Running out of regular medicine is the real danger. See "Diabetes when the power is out" for the same planning logic.
